    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Springdale, AR

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Hard freezes hit Arkansas most winters, especially in the northern and higher areas, and exposed pipes can split during a sudden cold snap. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to shield the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.
