    Pressure Switch Replacement in Springdale

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Springdale, AR

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    Control Box and Electrical Repair in Springdale, AR

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. Severe storms and heavy spring rain are common in Arkansas, and saturated ground can overwhelm sump pumps and flood sewer lines. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Jet Pump Repair in Springdale, AR

    Jet pumps, usually installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Springdale is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating smoothly again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.
