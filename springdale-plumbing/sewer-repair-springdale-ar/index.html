    Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Springdale

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage. We then use hydro-jetting or mechanical snaking to clear the obstruction, restoring proper flow to your system and preventing further damage. This approach is highly effective for the stubborn clogs often found in older Arkansas homes.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Springdale, AR

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from storms, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. DGR Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, making sure your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Springdale

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to repeat clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Springdale, AR

    A burst or collapsed sewer pipe can lead to significant damage and unsanitary conditions. These issues can result from shifting soil, corrosion of old cast iron or clay pipes, or heavy vehicle traffic above the line. DGR Plumbing offers emergency repair services to promptly address these critical failures. We precisely locate the damaged section and provide durable repair or replacement options, often using trenchless methods where appropriate to limit disruption.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Springdale

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Arkansas, attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining solutions to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Springdale

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Springdale, AR

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Springdale.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Springdale, AR

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Homes here are split between crawlspace and slab construction, and crawlspaces allow easier access for repairs and repiping. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
