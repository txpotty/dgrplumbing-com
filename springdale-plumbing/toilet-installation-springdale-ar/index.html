    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Springdale, AR

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Springdale

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Springdale

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks. Often the broken unit comes out and a 12 inch rough-in replacement goes in during the same trip, round or elongated. Close the stop until then.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Springdale, AR

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Springdale, AR

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. The old unit comes out in about twenty minutes. Most of the remaining time goes into cleaning the flange and setting the new bowl on shims so it does not rock on a floor that has settled over the years. We haul the old one away.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Springdale

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Springdale

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Springdale, AR

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Springdale, AR

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor. We bring the flange up with a stacking extender or replace it outright, and if you are moving the toilet on a slab, that means cutting concrete to reroute the drain and patching it afterward. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.
