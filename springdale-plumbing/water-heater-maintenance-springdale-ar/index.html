    Relief Valve and Safety Testing in Springdale, AR

    The relief valve is the tank's last line of defense. We lift the lever, confirm water discharges and the valve reseats without dripping, and check that the discharge pipe runs full size to an approved spot within a few inches of the floor or outside. Leaky valves get replaced. We check the expansion tank too.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Springdale

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. We descale through the service valves. Yearly descaling is standard advice for hard water.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Springdale

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Bad tanks need a few rounds.

    Yearly Service Plans in Springdale, AR

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. We track it for you. It pays off over time.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Springdale

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. We swap worn ones. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in Springdale

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Poor flames mean dirty burners, so we clean them, test the sensor and check the draft. Bad venting is dangerous. The gas line and shutoff get leak tested.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Springdale, AR

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.
