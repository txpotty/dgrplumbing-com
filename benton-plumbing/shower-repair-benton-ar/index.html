    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Benton, AR

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Benton

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Benton, AR

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Commercial Shower Repair in Benton, AR

    Gyms and apartment buildings run showers dozens of times a day. Commercial valves get heavy-duty cartridges, and we schedule around your busy hours so the showers stay open when people need them. Vandal-resistant heads are available. Limit stops are set to code on each valve.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Benton, AR

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. A meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan show whether the leak is the valve, the pan or the drain. The source gets repaired. With slab floors, the water can travel under the flooring of nearby rooms.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Benton, AR

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open.     Hot Water Pressure Problems in Benton, AR

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. We test both sides. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Benton

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. We pull the trim and look. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.
