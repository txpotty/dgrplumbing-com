    Water Line Repair We Handle in Benton, AR

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Benton

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. Then the new pipe gets pulled through underground. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Benton, AR

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Benton

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Benton

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. We confirm it in about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and continued movement tells us the leak is in the buried line. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Benton, AR

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Water pouring into a yard undermines everything around it. DGR Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. Most breaks are shut down quickly. If the repair will run long, a temporary bypass keeps water flowing to the house during the repair.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Benton, AR

    Besides repairs, DGR Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, for example when a restaurant adds fixtures or a gym adds showers. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size it to the fixture load. Then we coordinate with the water provider on the tap and meter change, set the backflow assembly and get the inspection signed off, so the space can open on schedule.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Benton

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes across Arkansas mostly use PEX and PVC, which resist corrosion but still benefit from proper support and freeze protection. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Benton, AR

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Two or more leaks on galvanized is a replacement. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Benton

    Spot repairs on an accessible line usually run $350 to $1,500. The deeper the pipe, the higher the price. A trenched meter-to-house replacement is typically $1,800 to $5,500, and trenchless replacement often lands between $3,000 and $7,000 because of the equipment involved. The written price comes before we start.

    Often the pipe itself is not covered. Resulting damage sometimes is. Some homeowners carry separate service line coverage through their insurer or the water provider, and that coverage usually pays for the line repair. We provide photos and an itemized invoice for your claim.

    Most replacements get Type K copper or PEX. For pulled lines we use HDPE. Copper lasts a very long time and resists punctures, while PEX handles ground movement and freezing better and costs less to install. We recommend one based on your soil, line length, budget and what your local code allows in Benton.

    Small spot repairs often do not need one. Full replacements usually do. Rules vary by jurisdiction in Benton, so DGR Plumbing checks the local requirement for your address and pulls the permit when one applies. An inspection happens with the pipe still exposed.

    Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see harder water from groundwater sources. Homes here are split between crawlspace and slab construction, and crawlspaces allow easier access for repairs and repiping. Tree roots wrap and crush shallow lines over time, and scale inside older pipe slowly narrows the opening until pressure drops and the wall gives out. Old galvanized is the top culprit.

    Type K copper, burial-rated PEX or HDPE poly. Each has its place. Copper resists punctures and lasts decades, PEX flexes with the soil and HDPE is what we use for trenchless pulls. We recommend one after looking at your soil, line length and budget.

    Spot repairs usually take a few hours. A full replacement takes about a day. Jobs with a deep line, a lot of hardscape or a trenchless pull can stretch to two or three days, mostly because of restoration work and inspection timing. We give you a time estimate with the quote.

    In most places the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. You own the rest. Some providers draw the line at the curb stop instead, so we confirm where the leak sits before quoting anything. If it turns out to be theirs, we tell you and help you report it.
