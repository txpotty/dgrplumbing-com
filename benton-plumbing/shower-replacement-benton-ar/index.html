    Replacing Showers for Hard Water Issues in Benton, AR

    Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see more mineral-heavy water from groundwater sources. Hard water can cause mineral buildup in showerheads, valves, and on surfaces, leading to reduced water pressure and a dingy appearance. If mineral scale has damaged your shower beyond repair, DGR Plumbing can replace affected components or the entire unit. We can also recommend solutions like water softeners to prevent future issues, helping your new shower in Benton, AR maintain its performance and looks for years to come.

    Walk-In Shower Conversions in Benton, AR

    Converting a traditional tub or shower combo into a spacious walk-in shower is a popular upgrade, especially for improving accessibility and modernizing a bathroom. Our team handles all necessary plumbing changes, including relocating drains and adjusting water lines to fit the new layout.     Shower Pan Replacement in Benton, AR

    A cracked or leaking shower pan can damage subfloors, walls and nearby rooms. On slab homes, water can travel under flooring and cause staining and odors. We remove the old pan, repair the drain and install a new pan with proper slope and waterproofing. A flood test proves the new pan is watertight.

    Shower Valve Replacement and Upgrades in Benton

    A leaky or malfunctioning shower valve can waste water and cause temperature swings. DGR Plumbing offers expert shower valve replacement, upgrading old, corroded valves with modern, reliable models. We can install pressure-balancing valves to prevent scalding, or thermostatic valves for precise temperature control. This service often coincides with a full shower replacement but can also be a standalone upgrade to improve your existing shower's performance and safety.

    Showerhead and Trim Upgrade in Benton, AR

    If the valve is sound, replacing the showerhead and trim can refresh the look without opening the wall. We match trim kits to your existing valve brand and install new heads, handhelds and slide bars. Hard water clogs older heads quickly, so we recommend models with easy-clean nozzles. This is the most affordable upgrade.

    Fiberglass Shower Unit Replacement in Benton

    Older fiberglass and acrylic units can crack, fade and leak at the base. We remove the old unit, check the framing and drain and install a new one-piece or multi-piece unit. Many homes have narrow doorways, so we often use multi-piece units that fit through. The result is a clean, durable shower.

    Shower Replacement Cost Comparison in Benton, AR

    A valve-only replacement usually costs far less than a full rebuild, but it only makes sense if the pan and walls are sound. Shower pan replacement often requires removing tile, which raises the cost. We compare the price of each option against the condition of your shower. That helps you invest in the fix that lasts.

    Tub-to-Shower Replacement in Benton

    Replacing an old tub with a walk-in shower improves safety and accessibility. We remove the tub, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. We can add a handheld shower and grab bar blocking. This upgrade is a common choice for aging in place.
