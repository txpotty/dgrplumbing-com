    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Benton, AR

    Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, but it cannot fix a crack, a belly or a collapsed section. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera will often show why. We compare the yearly cost of maintenance cleanings to a spot repair, lining or replacement. Sometimes regular cleaning is the sensible move for a few years. Other times a repair now saves more over time, and we show you the math.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Benton

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common culprits in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs. A camera inspection then shows where roots are entering so you can decide on lining or repair.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Benton, AR

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a stubborn crust. Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and clog aerators and water heaters. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Benton, AR

    Before any major sewer cleaning, especially for recurring issues, a sewer camera inspection is crucial. DGR Plumbing uses waterproof cameras to travel through your sewer lines, providing a live view of the pipe's interior. This lets us precisely locate the exact cause and position of a blockage, identify pipe damage like cracks or collapses, and choose the most effective cleaning method, saving time and avoiding unnecessary digging in Benton, AR.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Benton, AR

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Houses built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s may contain polybutylene supply lines, which are likely to fail without warning. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Benton, AR

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Benton
