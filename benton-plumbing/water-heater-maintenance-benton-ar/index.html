    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Benton

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Benton

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Benton, AR

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Benton

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Benton, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.

    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Benton

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup. Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and foul aerators and water heaters. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent early failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills in check.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Benton, AR

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets swapped out. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.
