    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Benton

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and common code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. DGR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is protected.

    Flushing a traditional tank water heater once a year is recommended in most homes. Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and foul aerators and water heaters. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from damaging heating elements.

    Leaks at the base, rusty water, rumbling noises, inconsistent hot water and an age over 10 years are common signs. A leaking tank should be replaced right away.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take more time.

    Manufacturer tank warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    While it might seem tempting, water heater replacement involves gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure and heavy equipment. It is a complex job best left to licensed professionals to ensure safety, proper installation and compliance with local plumbing codes, preventing potential leaks or hazards.
