    Main Water Line Repair in Rogers

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause serious water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team focuses on diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to pinpoint the exact location of the problem, limiting excavation and disruption to your property in Rogers.     Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Rogers

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Older homes in Arkansas often still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, both of which corrode from the inside with age. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Rogers is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Rogers

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before backfill.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Rogers

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Rogers

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process usually takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Rogers, AR

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades.
