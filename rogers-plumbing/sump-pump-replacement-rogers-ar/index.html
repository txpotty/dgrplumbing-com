    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Rogers

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    DGR Plumbing provides warranty coverage on both the sump pump unit itself, per the manufacturer's warranty, and our workmanship. We back our installations for your peace of mind and a reliable, long-lasting solution.

    Most sump pump replacements take one to two hours. Battery backups or new discharge lines take a bit longer.

    We install Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne pumps. Cast iron models are preferred for durability.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, is a secondary system that kicks on if the primary pump fails, loses power or cannot keep up with heavy water inflow. Many Rogers homes are better off with a backup system due to storm risks.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements typically do not require a permit.     To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by DGR Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.
