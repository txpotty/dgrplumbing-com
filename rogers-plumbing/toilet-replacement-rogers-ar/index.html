    What a Standard Swap Includes in Rogers

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. The base gets caulked, with a small gap left at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Rogers

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Rogers

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Rogers, AR

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Rogers, AR

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. We confirm the clearances before ordering. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Toilet Flange Repair During Replacement in Rogers

    Pulling the old toilet is the only chance to see the flange, and a cracked plastic ring or a rusted cast iron flange will ruin a new install within a year. It gets checked on every replacement. A split ring gets a stainless repair ring, while a flange that sits below the finished floor gets a spacer or extender so the seal compresses properly.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Rogers

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Rogers, AR

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.
