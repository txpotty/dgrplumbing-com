    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Rogers, AR

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rogers, AR

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Rogers, AR homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Rogers

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. When a tank starts leaking at the base, making loud popping sounds or giving rusty hot water, we replace it like-for-like or resize it, and the whole job usually takes two to four hours from shutoff to first hot shower. Hot water by evening.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Rogers

    We use Bradford White often on our jobs because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When we replace one, we match the fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty might still cover the tank, and register the new unit so the warranty is on file from day one. We file the warranty.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Rogers

    Houses built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s may contain polybutylene supply lines, which are likely to fail without warning. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Rogers

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. We price both options.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Rogers, AR

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Rogers, AR

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. We install both, and because parts overlap, a thermostat or element is easy to find when repairing a newer tank beats replacing it. Repair stays an option.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Rogers, AR

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Rogers, AR. Tankless and hybrid units cost more up front but can lower operating costs, and tankless often lasts 15 to 20 years versus about 8 to 12 for a tank. Ask for each option.
