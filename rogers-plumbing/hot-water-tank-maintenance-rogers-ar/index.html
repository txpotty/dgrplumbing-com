    The temperature and pressure relief valve is the part that keeps a tank from becoming dangerous.     Standard Hot Water Tank Flushing in Rogers

    Sediment buildup is a common issue in hot water tanks, especially where water hardness runs high.     Attic and Garage Tank Checkups in Rogers, AR

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Rogers

    A standard flush and inspection usually runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement typically adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    DGR Plumbing services all major brands of hot water tanks, including but not limited to Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State, Noritz and Bosch. Our technicians are familiar with a wide range of models and their specific maintenance needs.

    Our technicians are trained to service all major hot water tank brands, including Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, State, Kenmore and more. We have the experience to handle various models, whether gas or electric.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it lengthens the lifespan of your unit.

    Watch for slower recovery, rusty or cloudy hot water, noises, moisture around the base or a relief valve that drips. Any of these means it is time for an inspection.

    Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and foul aerators and water heaters. Mineral content contributes to sediment buildup in hot water tanks, which reduces efficiency and can damage heating elements or tank linings over time. Regular flushing is particularly important in hard water areas.

    Many manufacturers, including Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White, expect regular maintenance and can reject claims for sediment damage. Keeping service records protects your warranty.
