    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Rogers, AR

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Rogers, AR

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are set up to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can show up as wet spots or high water bills in your Rogers, AR home. We have specific methods for each.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Rogers
