    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rogers, AR

    Electric tankless heaters make sense in Rogers, AR homes without gas, or for a single sink or a guest bath far from the main heater. A whole-home electric unit draws heavy amperage.     Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Rogers, AR

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. The vent changes as well. Both are on the estimate.

    Propane Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rogers

    Propane homes in Rogers can run tankless units too, using a model built or converted for propane. Since propane runs at different pressure from natural gas, the regulator, line sizing and gas valve must all match, and the supplier may need to swap the tank regulator. Tank size matters too.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Rogers

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. The built-in pump can move hot water toward a distant bath on a timer, cutting the wait at the tap without a separate pump. Navien vents in PVC. Condensing units also need a condensate drain.
