    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Rogers

We also handle ADA upgrades. That includes setting fixtures at the right heights, adding blocking for grab bars and wrapping exposed drain lines under lavatories.

    Grease Trap Installation in Rogers

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Rogers

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Expansive clay soils across much of Arkansas swell and shrink with rainfall, which can stress foundations and crack buried sewer lines. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.

    Emergency Commercial Plumbing in Rogers

    A burst supply line above a drop ceiling can soak inventory and ceiling tile in under an hour. Shut the main first. We can talk your staff through the shutoff while the plumber drives over, then isolate the broken branch and get the rest of the building back on water. Sewage backups get the same response. We clear the main, camera it and tell you whether it will happen again.

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more.     Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Rogers

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away.     Commercial Water Heater Service in Rogers, AR

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.
