    Catch Basin Cleaning in Rogers

    Sumps and catch basins trap sediment. They need regular emptying. When a basin fills, sediment flows into the pipes and clogs them, so we vacuum out the basin, remove trash and debris and check the outlet pipe, and we schedule HOAs and commercial lots before and after the rainy season.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in Rogers

    Channel drains catch water at driveways and garage doors. Sand packs them quickly. Our tech pulls the grates, clears the channel and jets the outlet, replacing damaged grates on the same visit, which keeps water out of the garage during a downpour.
