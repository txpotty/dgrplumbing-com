    How We Replace Polybutylene in Rogers, AR

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Rogers, AR

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. Our crews use Uponor PEX-A and full-bore fittings. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance, and most homes are completed in two to four working days with water back on each night.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Rogers

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Insurance and Home Sale Documentation in Rogers

    Some insurers decline or surcharge policies on homes with polybutylene. Lenders may ask as well. After replacement, we provide the permit record, the invoice and photos of the new system, which is the paperwork insurance agents and buyers want when they ask whether the poly is gone.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Rogers, AR

    Many poly-era homes also have a blue or black polybutylene service line running from the water meter to the house. Buried poly service lines split, run up bills and saturate the yard. Utilities get marked first. Then we trench the route, install new PEX or copper service pipe and connect it to a new main shutoff at the house.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Rogers

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. Check the heater first. You may also see it under sinks, near the main shutoff or coming through the garage wall, and we trace runs through the attic to confirm how much of the house it serves.

    Affordable Polybutylene Solutions in Rogers, AR

    Poly replacement is a big expense, so we give you options instead of a single take-it-or-leave-it number. PEX-A usually costs less than copper.     Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Rogers, AR

    Some homeowners in Rogers, AR want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes, and join it with lead-free solder or press fittings. Expect a higher price and a slightly longer job with copper, in exchange for good heat and UV tolerance and a long history.

    Emergency Polybutylene Burst Repair in Rogers

    Poly fittings tend to let go during sharp temperature swings, when the pipe expands and contracts more than usual. Hard freezes hit Arkansas most winters, especially in the northern and higher areas, and exposed pipes can split during a sharp cold snap. If a line bursts in your house, close the main shutoff, then call us so we can stop the leak, make a temporary repair and restore water. A full replacement comes next.
