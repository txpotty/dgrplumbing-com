    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Rogers

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. A standard tank sits around 0.60 to 0.65. Condensing models top 0.80, and some qualify for utility rebates where those are offered.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Rogers

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. Model choice follows your hot water use. The install meets current code.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rogers

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. We drain the old tank, cap the gas, and set the new heater on a stand or pan. New flex connectors every time. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Rogers

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Rogers

    Bradford White tanks are sold only through plumbing wholesalers, not big-box stores, and plenty of Arkansas plumbers install them for that reason. Parts are easy to get. We follow their venting and clearance specs to the letter, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Rogers

    Closed systems are the reason expansion tanks exist. A pressure regulator or backflow preventer on the main stops heated water from pushing back to the street, so pressure climbs every time the burner runs. Code commonly calls for an expansion tank. We set its air charge to match house pressure.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rogers, AR
