    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Rogers

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can strain your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a correctly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.
