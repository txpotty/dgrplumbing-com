    Common signs of a frozen pipe include a sudden loss of water pressure, no water coming out of a faucet, or unusual noises like gurgling or banging from your pipes. You might also see visible frost on exposed pipes or notice a damp spot on a wall or ceiling if a slow leak has begun.

    While any pipe can freeze, older galvanized steel or copper pipes in uninsulated areas are often more susceptible to bursting due to their rigid material.     A frost-free hose bib has a long stem that shuts off water inside the warm part of the wall. Brands like Woodford and Prier make reliable models. They greatly reduce freeze damage at outdoor faucets.
