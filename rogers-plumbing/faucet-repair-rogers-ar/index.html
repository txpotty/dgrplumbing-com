    What We Fix on Faucets in Rogers, AR

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Rogers

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Rogers

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Rogers

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Rogers

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Rogers

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Hard freezes hit Arkansas most winters, especially in the northern and higher areas, and exposed pipes can split during a sharp cold snap. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Rogers

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. First we identify which type of valve you have. It matters. We carry the common washers, seats and cartridges, so the fix usually happens on the first trip.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Rogers

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Rogers, AR

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Many brands sell the handle on its own. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.
