    Common signs of hard water include soap scum buildup on fixtures and shower doors, dry skin and hair, dull laundry and scale buildup inside pipes and appliances like water heaters. We can perform a water hardness test in your Rogers home to confirm your need for a softener.

    Maintenance mainly involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, typically every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness.     A properly sized and installed water softener should not noticeably affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could point to an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.

    Homeowners with a loop can sometimes install a softener, but proper drain air gaps, bypass valves and programming matter. Professional installation helps avoid leaks and poor performance.

    While some homeowners attempt DIY installation, it's generally recommended to hire a professional. Professional installation provides proper sizing, correct connections and adherence to local plumbing codes.
