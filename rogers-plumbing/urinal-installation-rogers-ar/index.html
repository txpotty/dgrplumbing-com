    Waterless Urinal Installation in Rogers

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Sloan Flush Valve Installation in Rogers, AR

    Sloan Royal valves are on most commercial urinals we see. Each Sloan Royal gets the right inside parts for the bowl, a tuned control stop and a rebuild kit on hand. Optima sensor valves fit the same spud. Battery models need no wiring.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Rogers, AR

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Rogers, AR

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Sensor valves add a few hundred. We itemize each piece.

    Urinal Upgrades and Water Savings in Rogers, AR

    Older urinals often use 1.5 gallons or more per flush. A low-flow bowl and matching valve on the existing carrier cut water use sharply. Ask your water utility about rebates. We provide the model numbers you need.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Rogers

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. The flush handle has to be easy to reach. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Rogers, AR

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.
