    Foundation Leak Detection in Rogers

    Accurate detection is the key step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to confirm a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps verify hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Rogers, AR

    Crawl space foundations can develop leaks from plumbing, groundwater, or HVAC condensation. These leaks can lead to wood rot, mold, and structural damage if not handled quickly. Our team carefully inspects the crawl space and identifies the source of water intrusion. We then put in place solutions such as pipe repair, sump pump installation, or vapor barrier replacement to dry out and protect your crawl space in Rogers, AR from future moisture issues.

    Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in Rogers, AR

    In some cases, the most effective way to repair a foundation leak is by tunneling beneath the slab. This method allows our plumbers to reach the damaged pipe directly without breaking through your home's interior flooring. DGR Plumbing is experienced in safe and efficient tunneling, minimizing disruption to your Rogers, AR home and delivering a complete repair of the damaged pipe section.

    Water Line Reroute in Rogers

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This skips breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We typically use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for easy patching.

    Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Rogers

    Slab leaks are notorious for being difficult to detect, often showing quiet signs like warm spots on the floor or a sudden jump in your water bill. DGR Plumbing uses acoustic and infrared technology to accurately locate these hidden leaks under your concrete foundation. Once found, we offer tailored repair solutions, whether it's a spot repair, re-routing, or pipe bursting, to restore your plumbing system in Rogers without extensive damage to your property.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Rogers, AR

    Leaks in drain lines under the foundation can be harder to detect because they are not under constant pressure. We use camera inspection and hydrostatic testing to locate cracks and separated joints. Repairs may involve tunneling, cutting the slab or pipe lining. We explain the trade-offs of each method.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Rogers, AR

    For homes with basements in Rogers, AR, wall leaks can be a real concern. We offer comprehensive basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, injecting cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures prevent water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.

    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Rogers, AR

    Many older homes in Rogers, AR have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see more mineral-heavy water from groundwater sources. When these copper pipes develop pinhole leaks or cracks, they need specialized repair. Our technicians repair or replace sections of damaged copper piping, providing a durable seal that holds up to pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.
