    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in North Little Rock, AR

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a clear thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in North Little Rock.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in North Little Rock, AR

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of recurring slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in North Little Rock

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in North Little Rock

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak without risk or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in North Little Rock, AR

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know exactly where to listen.

    Electronic Leak Detection in North Little Rock

    For hard-to-find leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in various pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in North Little Rock homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.

    Warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water with fixtures off, a moving water meter and an unexplained jump in your water bill are common signs. Cracked tile, damp carpet and mildew odors also point to a slab leak. Call us if you notice any of these.

    Rocky, shallow soil in hilly parts of Arkansas makes trenching harder and can raise the cost on sewer and water line replacements. Hard water can also corrode copper pipes from the inside.     Slab leaks can cause serious damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials and increased humidity levels. Over time, it can lead to structural instability and major, costly repairs.
