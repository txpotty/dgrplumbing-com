    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in North Little Rock

    Many older homes in North Little Rock still have sewer lines made from outdated materials like clay or cast iron, which are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root invasion. We offer upgrades to modern, more durable materials such as PVC or HDPE (high-density polyethylene). These materials stand up to corrosion, roots, and shifting soil, giving your sewer system a much longer lifespan.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in North Little Rock

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that snags waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting options. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in North Little Rock, AR

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem.     Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in North Little Rock, AR

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. The method works well for many homes in North Little Rock and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.
