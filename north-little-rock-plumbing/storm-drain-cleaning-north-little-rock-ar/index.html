    Root Removal from Storm Drains in North Little Rock

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in North Little Rock, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. DGR Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can recommend further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in North Little Rock

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can handle a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and level.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in North Little Rock, AR

    Many homes connect downspouts to buried pipe that carries water away from the foundation. These lines clog with roof granules, leaves and nests, then back up and dump water at the wall. We jet them clear and test with a hose to verify flow at the outlet. For flat roofs with scuppers, we check that the discharge path is open too. Keeping water away from the slab helps protect against soil movement and settling.

    Channel and Trench Drain Cleaning in North Little Rock

    Channel drains across driveways and garage entries catch water before it reaches the house. Their narrow channels fill quickly with sand, leaves and debris. We remove the grates, clean the channel and jet the outlet pipe. Cracked or loose grates can be replaced while we are there. A clean trench drain keeps water out of your garage during a downpour.

    Catch Basin Cleaning in North Little Rock, AR

Regular cleaning of catch basins is essential to prevent them from becoming overloaded and causing blockages further down the system. Our service includes removing built-up leaves, sediment, trash, and other debris from catch basins, so they work correctly and protect your entire drainage system. This preventative maintenance is key to heading off larger issues.
