    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in North Little Rock

    Sizing a home unit comes down to two numbers: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. With a shower at about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen tap a bit under, two showers and a sink together need around 5 or 6 gallons per minute at the needed rise. That narrows the model list fast.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in North Little Rock

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. Less water goes down the drain.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in North Little Rock, AR

    Upfront, a tankless install in North Little Rock, AR costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. The gap shrinks over time. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. We price both side by side.
