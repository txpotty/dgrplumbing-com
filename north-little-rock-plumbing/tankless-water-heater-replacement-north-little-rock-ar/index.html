    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Old service valves often get replaced too.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in North Little Rock

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. We check meter capacity and pipe length against the manufacturer's sizing chart, then confirm pressure with the unit running. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in North Little Rock

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. Send us a photo of the display. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in North Little Rock

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Replacement in North Little Rock

    Rinnai units we pull are usually 12 to 18 years old, and the failure tends to be a leaking heat exchanger or a dead control board. A current Rinnai often mounts on the same bracket. That keeps wall patching small. Venting stays the same, or switches to PVC if you move to a condensing unit.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in North Little Rock

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Rinnai Condensing Upgrades in North Little Rock, AR

    Rinnai Sensei condensing models replace older non-condensing Rinnai units in a lot of homes, but the venting changes from stainless to PVC. The vent swap is part of our job. Gas and water connections usually line up.

    Navien Venting and Install in North Little Rock

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.
