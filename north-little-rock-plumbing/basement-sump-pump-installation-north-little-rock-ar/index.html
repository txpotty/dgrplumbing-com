    New Sump Pit Excavation in North Little Rock

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by locating the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor stays clean and level.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in North Little Rock

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels usually need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm alerts building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a simple maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    Sump Pump Pit Installation in North Little Rock, AR

    A properly built sump pit is fundamental to an effective sump pump system. If your basement lacks a suitable pit or needs an upgrade, DGR Plumbing provides professional sump pit installation. We precisely excavate and prepare the pit to the correct dimensions and depth for proper drainage and pump function. This foundational step is critical for any new sump pump installation and prevents the sediment buildup that can damage the pump.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in North Little Rock, AR

    A high water alarm is an inexpensive part that gives you warning before water reaches the floor. We install standalone alarms or Wi-Fi monitors that send a text if the water level rises, the pump fails or power drops. These are popular with homeowners who travel or own second homes. We test the float trigger in front of you so you know what the alarm sounds like.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in North Little Rock, AR

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are strong and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in North Little Rock, AR will install your submersible pump with precision, making sure of proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in North Little Rock

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which saves wear on the motor. Crawlspace pipes, exterior wall lines, and outdoor faucets are the usual freeze failures, so insulation and vent covers make a real difference. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the discharge.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost Comparison in North Little Rock, AR

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit is the least expensive option, typically a few hundred dollars plus the pump. Digging a new pit through the slab adds labor for saw cutting, excavation and concrete patching. Adding a battery backup increases the total but often costs less than one cleanup after a flooded basement. We give you an itemized quote so you can compare a basic primary pump against a full primary and backup system before we start.
