    Trenchless Water Line Repair in North Little Rock, AR

    A broken water service line wastes water and soaks the yard. It also drops pressure. We locate the break, then replace the line by bursting new HDPE through the old path or boring a new path, so the lawn and walkway stay put and you are back in water quickly. The new line gets pressure tested.

    Pipe Bursting in North Little Rock, AR

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Commercial Trenchless Pipe Repair in North Little Rock

    Commercial properties have sewer and storm lines under parking lots, sidewalks and buildings. Tearing it out costs plenty. Trenchless repair avoids that demolition and the disruption to tenants and customers, and we line larger diameter pipe and multiple laterals on a schedule that limits the impact on business.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in North Little Rock

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in North Little Rock

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your North Little Rock yard.

    Urgent Sewer Failures in North Little Rock

    Sewage backing into a tub is not a wait-and-see problem. Stop running water. We clear the main, scope it the same day and, if a liner or burst is the fix, book it as soon as possible while keeping the line flowing in the meantime so your household can still use the bathroom.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in North Little Rock

    Older North Little Rock homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Sectional Point Repair in North Little Rock

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in North Little Rock laterals.
