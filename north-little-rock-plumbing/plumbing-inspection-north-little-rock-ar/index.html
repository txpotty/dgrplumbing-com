    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue.     Hard Water Damage Assessment in North Little Rock

    Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see harder water from groundwater sources.
