    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in North Little Rock

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    Softener Loop Installation in North Little Rock

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We cut it in for you. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in North Little Rock

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. Our residential installs start with a test and a few questions about water use, then we pick a grain capacity that regenerates about once a week for your household. Weekly is the sweet spot. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in North Little Rock

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in North Little Rock

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Water Softener Replacement in North Little Rock, AR

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in North Little Rock

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in North Little Rock, AR

    Cost depends on the system type, its capacity and whether a loop already exists. An existing loop keeps the price down, and adding a loop adds labor and materials. Filters add cost. Your written quote lists equipment, labor and setup.
