    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in North Little Rock, AR

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main.     Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in North Little Rock

    We look at the whole line before recommending either. One offset joint near the house is a repair. Breaks every few feet, roots at every joint, or a belly that holds water for twenty feet mean the rest of the pipe is close behind, and in that case a full replacement saves you from paying to dig the same yard twice.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in North Little Rock

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind which leaves a single joint-free pipe in the ground. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in North Little Rock, AR

    Roots find sewer lines by following moisture. They need only a tiny gap. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease, and cutting them out with a machine only buys a few months, because the opening is still there. New PVC with glued joints gives them no way in.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in North Little Rock

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or tunnel under the slab from outside so the floors stay intact, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Homes here are split between crawlspace and slab construction, and crawlspaces allow easier access for repairs and repiping.
