    Sensor valves stop missed flushes and double flushes.     ADA Urinal Installation in Conway

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. We measure the rim twice. Inspectors measure too.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Conway

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Your custodial team needs training. We show them how.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Conway

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Conway

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Conway

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.
