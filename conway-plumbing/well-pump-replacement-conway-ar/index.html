    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Conway

    Old galvanized drop pipe can corrode and leak, causing pressure loss. Damaged wire can cause motor failure. When we pull the pump, we inspect both. We replace them with long-lasting materials like schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe as appropriate. This helps the new pump last for years.

    Remote properties and livestock wells often benefit from solar-powered pumps. Solar can be a practical option wherever a site gets steady sun.     Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Conway, AR

    Not every well failure means a bad pump. Failed capacitors, relays or pressure switches can stop the system. We test these components first and replace them if needed. This can save you the cost of pulling the pump. We explain what we find before making any recommendations.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Conway, AR

    Jet pumps are usually used for shallower wells and are located above ground, drawing water up through a suction pipe. Common issues include motor failure, impeller damage or pressure switch malfunctions. DGR Plumbing provides professional jet pump replacement with correct sizing and pressure tank integration.
