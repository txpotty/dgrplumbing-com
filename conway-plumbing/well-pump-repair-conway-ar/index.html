    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Conway, AR

    Many well pump issues come from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. Our licensed plumbers are also skilled in electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults.     Control Box and Electrical Repair in Conway, AR

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and relay. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are smart upgrades for homes that have taken on water before. Lightning and power surges often damage these components. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection and replace failed parts. A surge protector at the control box can help reduce repeat failures.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Conway, AR

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.
