    What Our Water Line Service Covers in Conway, AR

    PEX Water Line Repair in Conway, AR

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rodents chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Conway, AR

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. No water means no open sign. DGR Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, and we bring the larger fittings and couplings on the truck. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Conway, AR

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, since the utility is in charge of the meter and the pipe feeding it. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Conway, AR

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, setting up a temporary supply to the house if the job will take a while. Most breaks are fixed the same day. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Conway, AR

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind. We can clamp or replace a short section to get you through, which can hold for a while if the pipe is not too far gone. More often we recommend replacing the whole run from the meter to the house with copper or PEX. That fixes the pressure too.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Conway, AR

    Besides repairs, DGR Plumbing handles service line upgrades when a commercial tenant needs more flow, like a restaurant adding a dish line or a second restroom. Bigger meters need bigger pipe. We size it to the fixture load. Then we coordinate with the water provider on the tap and meter change, set the backflow assembly and get the inspection signed off, so the space can open on schedule.

    Underground Leak Detection in Conway, AR

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Some leaks barely make a sound. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, which finds leaks the microphone cannot hear. Rocky, shallow soil in hilly parts of Arkansas makes trenching harder and can raise the cost on sewer and water line replacements. You get a written price as soon as the leak is marked on the ground.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Conway

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Repair in Conway

    A minor leak repair often starts around $500. Replacing the whole line is a bigger number. A meter-to-house replacement or a trenchless pull can reach $5,000 or more on a long run or a deep line, and the price moves with pipe type, depth and whether we trench or bore. We quote it in writing after the inspection.

    Two to four hours for most spot repairs. Replacements usually take a day. Trenchless jobs are often a single day too, though digging the two pits and setting up the boring equipment takes a few hours on its own before any pipe moves. In most cases your water is back on before evening.

    Fix small leaks early. A drip becomes a gusher. Check your pressure once a year, since a failing regulator quietly pushes the line and every fixture harder than they were built for, and keep big-rooted trees off the pipe route. For older homes in Conway, replacing an aging galvanized line on your own schedule usually costs less than an emergency.

    Keep trees and shrubs with big roots well away from the line. Know where the pipe runs. Crawlspace pipes, exterior wall lines, and outdoor faucets are the usual freeze failures, so insulation and vent covers make a real difference. A pressure gauge on a hose bib tells you if the regulator is failing, and readings above 80 psi mean the line is under more stress than it should be. On galvanized lines, planning a replacement beats waiting for the break.

    In most places the water provider owns the meter and the pipe on the street side of it. You own the rest. Some providers draw the line at the curb stop instead, so before quoting we check exactly where the leak is. If it turns out to be theirs, we tell you and help you report it.

    Often the pipe itself is not covered. Damage it causes may be. Some homeowners carry separate service line coverage through their insurer or the water provider, and that coverage usually pays for the line repair. We provide photos and an itemized invoice for your claim.

    For PEX we use Uponor and Viega, depending on the job. Copper is Type K for buried runs. Couplings and transition fittings come from makers like Mueller and Ford, and we stick with fittings rated for direct burial in the soil conditions we find. We do not use cheap knockoff fittings.

    Yes, we stand behind it. A failed joint gets fixed at no charge. The workmanship warranty covers our labor on the repaired section, while the pipe and fittings carry their maker's warranty on top. All of it is listed on the service agreement you sign before we start.
