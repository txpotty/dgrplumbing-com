    Brands and Tank Materials in Conway, AR

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel. Homeowners near Conway on well water benefit from periodic testing and the right filter to protect pipes and appliances. Stainless steel connections hold up against corrosion from hard or aggressive water. We avoid tanks designed only for hydronic heating, since they are not approved for drinking water.

    Standard Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Conway

    Our standard installation service covers traditional tank-style water heaters, with the expansion tank correctly tied into your cold water supply line. We use high-quality, pre-charged diaphragm tanks designed to absorb thermal expansion efficiently. This keeps excessive pressure from building up in your system, which is crucial for prolonging the life of your water heater and preventing damage to fixtures and appliances. We always test the system thoroughly after installation to confirm proper performance and compliance with local Conway regulations.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Conway, AR

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installing.

    Tank Sizing and Placement in Conway

    Correct sizing and placement are key to an effective water heater expansion tank in Conway. An undersized tank will not provide adequate protection, while an oversized one is an unnecessary expense. We accurately calculate the required tank volume based on your water heater's capacity, system pressure and average water temperature. Placement matters too, usually near the water heater on the cold water inlet line, with easy access for future maintenance or inspection.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Conway

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is likely seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays steady.
