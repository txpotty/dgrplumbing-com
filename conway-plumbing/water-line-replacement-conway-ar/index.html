    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Conway, AR

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some home insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Worth replacing.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Conway, AR

    Pipe pulling works like this. We dig a pit at each end of the old line. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Conway, AR

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pack sand around it so rocks do not touch the pipe. Where it goes through a concrete wall, it gets a plastic sleeve and a seal to keep water and bugs out.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Conway

    Sometimes the open trench wins. A collapsed line, a route with sharp bends, or solid rock can rule out trenchless.     Water Line Materials We Install in Conway, AR

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job.     Copper Water Line Installation in Conway
