    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Conway

    A simple clog clearing starts around $150. Main line work costs more. Complex main line repairs can run past $3,000, depending on the drain involved, the extent of the damage and the access. We price it after we look.

    A trap swap or a fixture drain fix is usually an hour or two. Main lines take longer. Replacing a section of main line can take a full day or several days, with permits and digging as the main things that stretch it out. We give you the timeline up front.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, which is why recurring clogs usually call for a fix instead of another cleaning. A camera tells you which.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. So is water under the sink. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or water spots below a bathroom, point to a pipe problem instead of a simple clog. Catch it early.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. It cleans wall to wall. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, but a jet nozzle strips buildup off the entire pipe wall. We camera first on old or brittle pipe.

    We advise against them. Most only open a small hole. Harsh cleaners can soften old glue joints, eat at metal drain parts and sit in the trap if they do not clear the clog, which makes the job more dangerous for whoever opens the drain next. Mechanical cleaning is safer.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Pipe work takes longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack may run a day or longer with access and inspections setting the pace. The quote includes a time estimate.
