    Shower Replacement Options and Costs in Conway, AR

    A valve-only swap costs far less than a full rebuild. It only makes sense when the pan and walls are sound, though, because a new valve in a shower with a failing pan just moves the leak to a different spot. Pans usually mean tile removal. We match the option to the shower's condition.

    Showerhead and Trim Upgrade in Conway, AR

    If the valve is sound, new trim and a new head can refresh the shower without opening the wall. We match a trim kit to your existing valve brand, install a new head or handheld and add a slide bar if you want one. Easy-clean nozzles help with scale. This is the least expensive upgrade.

    Aging-in-Place Shower Replacement in Conway

    Aging in place often starts with getting rid of the tub. We remove it, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height, with blocking for grab bars and a handheld shower on a slide bar so the shower works seated or standing. Thresholds stay low. It is one of the safest changes you can make.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Conway, AR

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. We relocate the drain to fit the new pan, raise the valve to shower height and set the floor slope so water stays in the shower instead of running out the entry. Grab bar blocking is easy now. ADA layouts are available.

    Prefab Shower Replacement in Conway

    Prefab units past their useful life usually show it at the base. Fading, hairline cracks and a soft spot underfoot all mean water has been getting through, and we check the subfloor and the wall framing once the old unit is out. Then the new unit goes in. It goes on mortar with a sealed drain.

    Leaking Shower Pan Replacement in Conway, AR

    Leaking pans soak the subfloor and the ceiling below. Prefab or tiled, the failed pan comes out, the subfloor gets repaired and the new pan is flood-tested for 24 hours. We replace the drain as well. It ends the cycle.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Conway, AR

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. We take the old tile and pan down to the studs and subfloor, replace any damaged wood and install a new waterproofed pan and walls, then set the valve and drain to the finished tile depth. The slope has to be right. We flood-test before tile.

    Accessible Shower Replacement in Conway, AR

    Barrier-free showers need low or flat entries and controls within reach. We install the valve where someone seated can reach it, add blocking for grab bars and set a handheld on a slide bar, all following ADA guidelines when you ask for them. Entries stay wide. We plan for a seat.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Conway

    Shower replacement in Conway, AR runs from about $1,500 for a basic unit swap to over $10,000 for a custom tiled walk-in. Most of the spread comes from the shower type, the materials you pick and how much plumbing has to move, especially if the drain shifts on a slab. Tiled showers run higher. We quote after an on-site look.

    Houses built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s may contain polybutylene supply lines, which are likely to fail without warning. Galvanized supplies and old drain lines often need updating during a shower replacement. We inspect the existing plumbing and recommend the upgrades needed for good pressure and code compliance, so the new shower is not tied to failing pipe. It is better to fix it now.

    Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and foul aerators and water heaters. Even moderately hard water leaves deposits on heads and surfaces over time. That cuts flow and leaves stains, so when we replace a shower, we can suggest fixtures with easy-clean nozzles and finishes that resist spotting. Easy-clean heads are worth it.

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out and a new pan, walls and fixtures go in, with the drain moved as needed. Accessibility improves a lot.

    New valves and fixtures carry manufacturer warranties, often lifetime for the original owner. Our workmanship is guaranteed on every job.

    For most homes, Moen or Delta valves are hard to beat. For the unit or the base, Sterling and American Standard make durable acrylic and fiberglass options, and Kohler makes plenty of both valves and bases. Budget shapes the pick.

    If no one uses the tub, a walk-in shower is often safer and more convenient. Keep at least one tub in the home if resale to families matters to you.

    Most replacements wrap up in one to three days. Custom tile showers or jobs with a lot of plumbing rerouting can take three to seven days, especially if we find structural damage once the old shower is out. You get a schedule first.
