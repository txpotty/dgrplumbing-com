    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Conway

    Most poly replacements in Conway fall between $4,000 and $15,000 or more. Size drives the price. Small homes with one bath and an open attic cost the least, and big multi-bath houses or copper jobs cost the most. The quote comes in writing at the visit.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. We use Uponor most often. For copper, we install Type L tubing from established mills rather than the thinner Type M found in many older homes, because the thicker wall holds up better over time.

    Both work well in Arkansas homes. PEX bends, tolerates freezes and costs less. Copper is stiff, long lasting and fine in sun and heat, and we will help you choose based on the house, water and budget.

    Homeowners near Conway on well water benefit from routine testing and the right filter to protect pipes and appliances. Hard water does not cause poly to fail the way chlorine does. It adds scale. Scale buildup can reduce flow and add stress to pipe that is already brittle, so in hard water areas we often suggest adding a softener loop during the replacement.

    In most cases, yes, a plumbing permit is required for full repiping in Conway. Our office handles it. That includes the application, the rough-in inspection with the walls open and the final sign-off, so you do not have to deal with the paperwork or the scheduling.

    Look for gray, flexible plastic pipe at your water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff, often stamped PB2110. It is most common in homes from the late 1970s to mid 1990s.

    Properly installed PEX usually lasts 25 to 50 years. Copper often goes further. Type L copper can last 50 years or more in friendly water, and either one is a large step up from polybutylene.
