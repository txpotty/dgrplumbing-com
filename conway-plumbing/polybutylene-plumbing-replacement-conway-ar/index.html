    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Conway, AR

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance. Most homes are completed in two to four working days.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Conway, AR

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or belly. We replace these lines with PEX sized for manufactured homes and secure them properly underneath. We also check heat tape and insulation on exposed lines where winter freezes occur. Before a deep freeze, disconnect hoses, drip a faucet on outside walls, and know where your main shutoff is located. The result is a reliable, modern supply system.

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Conway

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written estimate for replacement.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Conway

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process. Our team specializes in full home repiping using PEX (cross-linked polyethylene), a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant material. PEX is an excellent choice for Arkansas homes, as it resists freezing better than rigid pipes and expands to accommodate temperature changes. We carefully plan the repipe to limit disruption, often running new lines through attics or existing wall cavities, delivering a complete and long-lasting upgrade for your entire plumbing system.
