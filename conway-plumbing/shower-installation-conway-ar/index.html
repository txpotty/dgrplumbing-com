    Custom Shower System Installation in Conway, AR

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. Layout comes first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Conway

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Conway

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Add a handheld and a couple of body sprays and you can be running more water than a standard 40-gallon water heater can keep hot for a long shower. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Conway

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. The valve goes in next. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Conway, AR

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Inspection comes next. Trim goes on once tile is done.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Conway

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. Replacing an old pan, converting a tub to a shower or swapping in a new valve and trim can make a big difference without tearing the bathroom down to the studs. We price the options side by side. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Conway, AR

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. Commercial installs get tamper-resistant trim and heads, plus drains sized for heavy simultaneous use. We work around your business. A master mixing valve can protect the whole room from scalding.

    Digital Shower Systems in Conway, AR

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.
