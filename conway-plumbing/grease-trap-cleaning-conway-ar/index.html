    Why Choose DGR Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Conway, AR?

    A grease trap works by slowing the water down long enough for fat to float and food to sink. Past about 25 percent full, the separation stops working. The grease then slips past the outlet into the lateral, cools into a hard white crust, and a cable machine only bores a narrow hole through it.

    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the rotten-egg odor that decaying grease gives off.
    • Flexible Scheduling, We work before opening or after close, so the kitchen keeps cooking.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.

    Citywide Coverage

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Conway

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. We pump each compartment on its own. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Emergency Grease Trap Cleaning in Conway, AR

    Sometimes the trap is fine and the problem is downstream. A camera tells us quickly.     Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Conway, AR

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.
