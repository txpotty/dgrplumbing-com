    The cost for copper pipe leak repair in Conway varies widely, typically ranging from $200 for a minor, accessible pinhole leak to over $2,000 for complex repairs like under-slab leaks requiring extensive work.     We typically use Type L copper, which has thicker walls than Type M and lasts longer. In some situations we use ProPress fittings or a PEX transition.

    Insurance frequently covers sudden water damage but not the cost of repairing the pipe itself. Review your policy, and we will provide documentation to help with a claim.

    Copper pipe leaks are often caused by corrosion, especially from hard or acidic water. Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and foul aerators and water heaters. Other causes include excessive water pressure, improper installation, shifting foundations putting stress on joints and freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Pinhole leaks are a common symptom of internal corrosion.

    Type K, L and M copper pipes differ mainly in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is usually used for less demanding applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    We work with all standard types and brands of copper piping, including Type K, L and M, which are commonly found in residential and commercial plumbing systems.
