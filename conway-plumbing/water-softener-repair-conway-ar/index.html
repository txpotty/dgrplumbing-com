    Hard Water Symptoms and Diagnostics in Conway

    Scale on faucets means trouble. DGR Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, looks at each part of the system and gives you a repair plan for your Conway home. We test hardness before and after the repair, so you see the grains per gallon fall.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Conway

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Conway, AR

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. DGR Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A ten-year-old unit with a sound tank and a rebuildable valve is usually worth fixing, while an obsolete head with no parts available is a different conversation. We lay out both options.

    Control Valve Repair in Conway

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. DGR Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Timing problems get fixed, whether mechanical or electronic. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Popular Water Softener Brand Repair in Conway

    GE, Whirlpool, Culligan, Kenmore, EcoPure and Morton make the popular softeners we repair most often, along with the private-label units from hardware stores. Every brand has its own valve layout and weak points. DGR Plumbing technicians know those designs, so diagnosis is quick. Make or model, the repair in your Conway garage is done right.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Conway, AR

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Conway, AR

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. Installing a new quality softener usually runs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. If the valve is obsolete and parts are scarce, replacing the head or the whole unit may make more sense than chasing used parts online.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Conway, AR

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.
