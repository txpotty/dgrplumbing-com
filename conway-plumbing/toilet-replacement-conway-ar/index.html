    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Conway, AR

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Measure to the closet bolts, not the baseboard. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Conway

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. Pair it with a grab bar if you can, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Conway, AR

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. We confirm the clearances before ordering. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Conway

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Conway

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then the leveling. We caulk the base except for a small gap at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Conway, AR

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They cost more and weigh more. Getting one up a staircase takes two people, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.
