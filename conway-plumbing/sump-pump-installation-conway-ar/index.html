    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Conway

    Replacing a pump in an existing pit usually costs $600 to $1,500. Installing a new pit with slab cutting and excavation often runs $1,500 to $4,000. Battery backups typically add $500 to $1,500.

    A well-maintained sump pump typically lasts about 7 to 10 years. However, its lifespan can vary based on usage frequency, water quality and pump quality. Regular inspections by a professional can help determine if your sump pump in Conway is nearing the end of its useful life.

    It's recommended to have your sump pump inspected and cleaned once a year, ideally before the rainy season. This maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit and testing the discharge line to ensure it's free of obstructions. Regular checks prevent surprise failures.

    Most standard sump pump installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, if a new sump pit needs to be excavated or if there are extensive plumbing or electrical adjustments, the process might take a full day. We always aim for efficiency without compromising quality.

    A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space. A pedestal sump pump has its motor mounted on a shaft above the pit, making it easier to access for maintenance and usually more affordable. Both types are effective, and our plumbers can help you choose the best fit for your Conway basement.

    A simple replacement often does not need a permit. New pits, electrical work or discharge connections may require one, and we will let you know.

    Most manufacturers offer warranties from 3 to 5 years, and some premium models carry longer coverage. We also warranty our installation workmanship.

    DGR Plumbing installs a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their durability and performance. Our common selections include Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty Pumps. We can recommend the best brand and model to suit your specific needs and budget for your Conway home.
