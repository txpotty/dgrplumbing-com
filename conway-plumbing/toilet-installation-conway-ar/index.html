    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Conway

    Labor to install a customer-supplied standard toilet usually runs $200 to $400. Including a mid-range toilet, most installs total $450 to $900, while wall-hung systems with carriers range from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. We give you a firm price up front.

    Common signs that you might need a new toilet include frequent clogs, constant running, visible cracks in the bowl or tank, excessive wobbling, or a toilet that is very old and inefficient. If you're always repairing it, replacement is often more cost-effective.

    Most standard toilet installations can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as wall-mounted or smart toilets, can take longer due to additional plumbing or electrical work.

    When choosing a new toilet, consider factors like bowl shape (round vs. elongated), height (standard vs. ADA-compliant), flushing system (gravity-fed vs. pressure-assisted), water efficiency (gallons per flush), and overall style to match your bathroom. We can help you navigate these choices.

    Generally, replacing an existing toilet with a similar model in the same location in Conway does not require a permit. However, if you are relocating a toilet, adding a new toilet, or making significant changes to the plumbing system, a permit may be required. We can advise you on local code requirements.

    DGR Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works correctly. Specific warranty details will be included with your service agreement.

    We are experienced in installing all major toilet brands, including Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, Gerber, and many others. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific requirements for each manufacturer to deliver a proper and efficient installation.
