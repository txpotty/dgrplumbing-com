    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Conway, AR

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They need no drain and no salt. We explain the differences honestly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Conway, AR

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Conway

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Conway

    A standard single tank softener replacement usually costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and proven performance.

    Requirements vary by city. Simple replacements may not require one, but new plumbing connections may. We check local rules on your behalf.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but change their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    Many systems include 5 to 10 years on the valve and longer coverage on tanks. We also provide a labor warranty.
