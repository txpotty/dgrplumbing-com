    Main Water Line Repair in Hot Springs

    The main water line brings water from the public supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Hot Springs, AR

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and clog aerators and water heaters. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Hot Springs

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided. Trenchless repair methods, like pipe bursting or pipe lining, allow us to fix or replace underground pipes with minimal disruption to your landscaping, driveway, or foundation. This method is often faster and less intrusive than conventional digging, making it a popular choice for homeowners in Hot Springs looking to preserve their property. We'll check whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Hot Springs, AR

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a simple spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with actual information.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Hot Springs

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Hot Springs, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our experienced plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section.     PEX Water Line Repair in Hot Springs, AR
