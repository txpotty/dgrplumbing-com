    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Hot Springs, AR

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. We help you pick.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Hot Springs

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. New flex connectors every time. Then we leak test with soap solution and check draft at the hood.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Hot Springs

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. We help you match model to demand. The install meets current code.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Hot Springs

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. The 1/2-inch line feeding a tank is rarely enough. The venting changes too. We check meter capacity and pipe length before quoting so there are no surprises halfway through the job.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Hot Springs, AR

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. A bigger burner needs more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Hot Springs, AR

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Hot Springs, AR

    Warranty length on a tank mostly tracks anode size and burner design, so a 12-year tank is a different build from a 6-year one. Some have self-cleaning dip tubes for sediment. Powered anodes outlast standard rods. We price it against how long you plan to stay.
