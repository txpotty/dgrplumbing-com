    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    PEX rarely fails on its own. Most failures trace back to a nail or screw through the pipe, a rodent working on an attic run, or a fitting that was not crimped fully. We carry the common sizes. Most emergency PEX repairs in Hot Springs, AR come down to a cut and a splice, usually under an hour after the wall is open.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Hot Springs

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. A shower valve set two inches too low or a vanity stub that misses the cabinet back means opening the wall again after the tile is up, which is expensive and slow. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Hot Springs

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside out. After 40 or 50 years the bore is so choked with scale that a half-inch line delivers a trickle, and the water comes out tinted brown after it sits overnight. A full PEX repipe replaces all of it. You get real pressure back, and the discolored water goes away with the old pipe.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Hot Springs

    PEX-B is stiffer, is cheaper by the roll, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Hot Springs

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Either one lasts for decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Hot Springs, AR

    Gray polybutylene was installed widely from the late seventies into the mid nineties. Chlorinated water breaks the pipe and its plastic fittings down from within, which means it can look perfectly fine until a fitting suddenly fails. Insurers ask about it. Swapping it for PEX can help with insurance and with resale.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Hot Springs, AR

    Price on a repipe depends on a short list: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Drywall patching may be separate. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Hot Springs, AR

A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.
