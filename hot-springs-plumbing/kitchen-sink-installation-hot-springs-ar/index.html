    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Hot Springs

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Hot Springs, AR

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Composite Sink Installation in Hot Springs

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. We tighten by hand first. Composite makers usually call for silicone instead of plumber's putty under the strainer, since some putties stain the material, and we follow that sheet.
