    Pipe Bursting Services in Hot Springs, AR

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is highly effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Hot Springs, AR.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Hot Springs

    Many homes built in the 1980s and 1990s may have polybutylene pipes, which are prone to sudden failure due to their material composition. If you have polybutylene in your underground water lines, proactive replacement is usually recommended. We focus on safely removing and replacing these problem pipes with modern, durable alternatives, preventing future leaks in Hot Springs.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Hot Springs

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Hot Springs

    Trenchless pipe repair offers a less invasive alternative to traditional excavation for buried pipe issues.     Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Hot Springs

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are usually temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Main Water Line Repair in Hot Springs

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks occur.
