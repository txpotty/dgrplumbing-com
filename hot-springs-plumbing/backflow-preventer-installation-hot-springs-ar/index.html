    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Hot Springs, AR, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Hot Springs

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it right away with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Hot Springs, AR

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Before a deep freeze, disconnect hoses, drip a faucet on outside walls, and know where your water shutoff is located. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.
