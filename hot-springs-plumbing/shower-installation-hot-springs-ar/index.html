    Shower Installation Work We Do in Hot Springs, AR

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Hot Springs, AR

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. Slab homes need concrete work. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Digital Shower Systems in Hot Springs, AR

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. The outlet is an electrician's job. We plumb the valve and outlets.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Hot Springs

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Then the rough-in inspection. We return for trim after tile.

    Shower Stall Installation in Hot Springs

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. Fiberglass and acrylic bases go down on a mortar bed so they cannot flex, and flex is the usual reason a base cracks early. The drain connects from below. We check the flow at the head once the trim is on.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Hot Springs

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. We add solid wood blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare, set the valve within reach of a seat and install a handheld on a slide bar at a height that works seated or standing. No high curbs. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.
