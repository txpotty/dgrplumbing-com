    Flow Control Device Repair in Hot Springs

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Hot Springs, AR

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. Once a riser cracks, rainwater and grit wash straight into the tank. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. Pedestrian lids in a drive lane are a liability.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Hot Springs, AR

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Over the years, hydrogen sulfide gas softens the concrete above the water line. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Hot Springs

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink. Once one cracks or falls loose, the trap is just a box that grease passes through. We pump it out and pull the old baffle. Plastic units get a factory replacement part. Steel traps with a rusted-out baffle may need a fabricated piece, and sometimes a new trap is the cheaper answer.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Hot Springs

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Hot Springs

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.
