    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Hot Springs

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clear. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Hot Springs, AR

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Hot Springs, AR. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Hot Springs

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Hot Springs, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.
