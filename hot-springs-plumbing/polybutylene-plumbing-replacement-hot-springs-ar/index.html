    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Hot Springs

    The cost for polybutylene plumbing replacement in Hot Springs can range from $4,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of your home, the chosen material (PEX is generally less expensive than copper), and the complexity of the installation, especially with slab foundations. We provide detailed, upfront estimates after a careful inspection.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty on the repipe, and pipe manufacturers offer their own long-term warranties. We explain coverage before we start.

    Most policies do not pay for replacing old pipe, though they may cover sudden water damage. Some insurers raise rates or refuse coverage for homes with polybutylene.

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water break down the plastic from the inside, making it brittle. Fittings and hot water lines often fail before the rest.

    Polybutylene plumbing replacement is a complex job that requires specialized tools, knowledge of plumbing codes, and experience with various piping materials and connection methods. Attempting a DIY replacement can lead to costly errors, future leaks, and may not pass inspections. It's best left to a licensed plumber.

    Properly installed PEX piping typically has a lifespan of 25 to 50 years, while copper piping can last 50 to 70 years or even longer. Both are significant upgrades from polybutylene and offer decades of reliable service for your Hot Springs, AR home.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A and Type L copper with Viega ProPress or soldered fittings. We recommend materials based on your home and water conditions.

    Both PEX and copper are excellent choices. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally more affordable for installation. Copper is very durable, lasts a very long time, and is resistant to UV light. We'll help you choose the best option for your Arkansas home and budget.
