    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Hot Springs, AR

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil around the slab or foundation.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Hot Springs, AR

    Many Hot Springs, AR homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. DGR Plumbing provides thorough maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system. This keeps your home protected even when the primary power source fails, a common concern during severe storms.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Hot Springs

    River flooding affects low-lying parts of Arkansas, and floodwater can contaminate wells and swamp drain fields. We test your pump ahead of the rainy season by filling the pit until the float trips. We watch the pump empty the basin, listen for grinding bearings and measure how fast it cycles. If the pump struggles, you still have time to fix it before the first big storm.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Hot Springs, AR

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Hot Springs, AR.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Hot Springs, AR

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff. We service these smaller units, clear their screens and confirm the discharge reaches daylight or an approved drain. Crawlspace pipes, exterior wall lines, and outdoor faucets are the usual freeze failures, so insulation and vent covers make a real difference.
