    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Hot Springs, AR

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more economical than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes more sense.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Hot Springs, AR

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is typically the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem. We explain the cost of repair versus replacement clearly. Call (501) 251-9708 to schedule a check.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Hot Springs

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Hot Springs, AR

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Arkansas. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components. We make sure all connections are leak-free and the drain line is properly routed. We will also provide guidance on initial setup and salt refills for lasting soft water.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Hot Springs

    Clack valves are a popular choice for their reliability and adjustable settings. If you have an older Clack system failing, DGR Plumbing offers expert Clack water softener replacement in Hot Springs. We can replace just the control valve or the entire system, ensuring compatibility with your existing setup and configuring the new unit for peak performance.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Hot Springs

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Hot Springs

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Hot Springs, AR

    Fleck is a well-known brand for dependable water softener control valves, found in many residential and commercial systems across Arkansas. If your Fleck-controlled softener is beyond repair or simply outdated, DGR Plumbing can expertly replace the entire unit. We are familiar with many Fleck models and can recommend an equivalent or upgraded system that fits seamlessly with your existing plumbing in Hot Springs.
