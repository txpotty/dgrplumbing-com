    Replacement and Upgrade Services in Hot Springs, AR

    Steel traps from decades back rust through at the baffle seams, and by then the outlet is usually passing grease straight to the sewer. A camera down the outlet line tells us how much damage the old unit already did. Sometimes the line needs jetting too. Upgrading also gives you a chance to move from a 20 GPM trap to a 35 or 50 if the menu or seat count grew.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in Hot Springs, AR

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. A trap 40 feet from the sink lets grease cool and stick in the pipe before it ever reaches the unit. Closer is better. For outdoor tanks we measure the hose run from where a pump truck can park.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Hot Springs

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. They cost more up front. They also need a dedicated electrical circuit, which we coordinate with your electrician before the unit arrives. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Hot Springs

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. We count each sink, floor drain and dish machine that will tie in, then add the gallons per minute each one can dump. That number decides between an indoor unit and an outdoor tank, and where it can go with clean pipe runs to the sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    Buffets, grocery delis and kitchens doing several hundred covers a night usually end up with an in-ground gravity interceptor of 1,000 gallons or more. The work is excavation and a level gravel bed, then the tank and its inlet and outlet piping. Homes here are split between crawlspace and slab construction, and crawlspaces allow easier access for repairs and repiping. We plan the dig and the backfill around what the soil report and the open hole actually show us. Risers bring the lids to grade.

    Commercial Grease Trap Installation in Hot Springs

We size the trap to that peak flow, not the average. Undersized traps are the usual failure.
