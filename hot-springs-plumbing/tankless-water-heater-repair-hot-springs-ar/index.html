    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Hot Springs

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup.     Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Hot Springs

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Hot Springs, AR

We clean or replace the rod, look for a cracked igniter or a lazy spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.
