    Grinder Pump Installation in Hot Springs

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Hot Springs

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. Venting still applies.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Hot Springs, AR

    Upgrading an old ejector does not always mean a new basin. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Hot Springs, AR

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin costs the least, often done in a few hours. A new system with a basin, slab excavation, venting through the roof, electrical work and an alarm costs more, but it is the only way to add a bathroom below the sewer line in most homes without a macerating unit. We price both options.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Hot Springs

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. The slab gets repoured.

    New Construction Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    On a new build, the basin goes in before the slab is poured. With no concrete to cut, it costs the least then, and we stub drains, vent and discharge to the plans for a clean rough-in inspection. It keeps the pour on schedule.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Hot Springs, AR

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Hot Springs

    Liberty ejector pumps are a common pick on our jobs, with cast iron bodies and two-inch solids handling on most sewage models. Liberty sells packaged systems with basin, lid, pump and check valve already matched, which means less field fitting and a listed assembly for inspection. We still add a shutoff.
