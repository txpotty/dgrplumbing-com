    Grease Line Jetting in Hot Springs

    Grease that escapes the trap often coats the line between the kitchen and the interceptor or the sewer. We use hydro jetting to scour the inside of the pipe and remove hardened grease. This prevents repeat backups and keeps your trap working as designed.

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Hot Springs

    Small hydromechanical traps under sinks fill quickly in busy kitchens and often need cleaning every few weeks. We remove the lid, pump or hand-scoop the grease and solids, and scrape the baffles. We check the gasket, flow control fitting and inlet for damage. Then we reassemble the unit and confirm water flows through correctly. This keeps odors down and prevents slow sink drainage.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Hot Springs, AR

    Large in-ground interceptors require a vacuum truck to fully pump out grease, water and settled solids. We open each compartment, pump it down and use high-pressure water to break up hardened grease on the walls. We inspect inlet and outlet tees, baffles and risers while the tank is empty. Warm weather can make grease odors stronger, so we also check lid seals. A complete pump-out restores full retention capacity.
