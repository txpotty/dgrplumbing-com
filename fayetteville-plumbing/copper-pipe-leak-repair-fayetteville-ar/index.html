    Pinhole Leak Repair in Fayetteville

    Pinhole leaks are a common issue with older copper pipes, often caused by corrosion or acidic water. Our technicians in Fayetteville specialize in accurately locating these small, elusive leaks, which can lead to significant water damage if left alone. We use precise tools to identify the exact spot and then perform a targeted repair, typically cutting out the damaged section and soldering in a new piece of copper pipe. This method creates a strong, durable seal, preventing future issues in that area.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Fayetteville, AR

    Beyond reactive repairs, DGR Plumbing also offers preventive maintenance services designed to extend the life of your copper pipes in Fayetteville, AR. This includes assessing water quality, inspecting for early signs of corrosion or wear and advising on water treatment solutions like water softeners to limit hard water effects. Regular inspections can identify potential weak spots before they turn into costly leaks, saving you money and stress in the long run and keeping your plumbing robust.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original workmanship, thermal expansion or vibration. A leaking joint often shows a white or green crust around the fitting. We clean, reheat and resolder the joint or replace the fitting entirely if it is corroded. The result is a clean, fully sweated connection that holds pressure.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    A burst copper pipe is a plumbing emergency that requires immediate attention, especially during hard freezes. Hard freezes hit Arkansas most winters, especially in the northern and higher areas, and exposed pipes can split during a sudden cold snap. When temperatures drop significantly, water inside pipes can freeze and expand, causing them to rupture. DGR Plumbing offers fast response for burst copper pipe repair in Fayetteville, AR, limiting water damage to your home. We quickly isolate the leak, remove the damaged section and replace it with new copper piping, restoring your system to full function and protecting it against future freezes.
