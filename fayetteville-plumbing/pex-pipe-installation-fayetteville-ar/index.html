    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Fayetteville

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    Whole-Home PEX Repiping in Fayetteville

    Low pressure at every faucet, across the whole house, usually points at the pipe and not the fixtures. In an older house that usually means rust-choked galvanized or polybutylene with tired fittings, and a new faucet cartridge will not touch it. A whole-home repipe will. Every line from the main valve to each angle stop gets replaced, and the system is tested before anything gets closed up.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Fayetteville, AR

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. In the cold, it takes longer to shrink back. Joints get time to set before we pressurize.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Fayetteville

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Homeowners near Fayetteville on well water benefit from routine testing and the right filter to protect pipes and appliances. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines.     Hydronic Heating with PEX in Fayetteville

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, though not the same pipe you drink from. Heating work calls for oxygen-barrier PEX, coated so oxygen cannot seep through the pipe wall and corrode the boiler, the circulator and the iron parts. Loop length matters as well. Too long and the far end runs cold.

    Uponor PEX System Installation in Fayetteville

    Uponor's ProPEX system is PEX-A joined with an expansion tool and a cold-expansion ring.
