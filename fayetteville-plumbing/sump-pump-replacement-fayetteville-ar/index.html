    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Fayetteville

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Fayetteville, AR

    A sudden sump pump failure can lead to immediate flooding, especially during heavy rainfall. DGR Plumbing offers emergency sump pump replacement in Fayetteville, AR to address urgent situations. Our rapid response team will diagnose the problem and replace your faulty pump with a new, reliable unit. We work to minimize water damage and restore your peace of mind as quickly as possible.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Fayetteville, AR

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Fayetteville, AR, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Fayetteville

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Fayetteville for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and improve efficiency. That is key for handling heavy rainfall.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Fayetteville

    Power outages during severe storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers important protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units. Our team helps you select a system with enough run-time to protect your Fayetteville home through extended power interruptions. That gives you peace of mind when the lights go out.

    The cost of a sump pump replacement in Fayetteville, AR can range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, its horsepower and the complexity of the installation.     Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a smart investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm typically runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to activate automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is important for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    It is recommended to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    While it may seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is strongly recommended.
