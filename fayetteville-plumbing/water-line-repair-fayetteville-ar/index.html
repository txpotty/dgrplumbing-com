    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Fayetteville

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We verify the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to locate the exact spot. A single section is cut out and replaced with repair couplings or a new length of pipe, and the line is pressure tested before we backfill.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Fayetteville

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off quickly in an emergency.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, find the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired the same day. We also document the damage with photos for your insurance or HOA.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Fayetteville, AR

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can loosen those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is responsible for the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve on the same trip.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Fayetteville, AR

    A single leak on a newer copper or PEX line is usually a quick spot repair. Repeat leaks, galvanized pipe or a line older than about 40 years usually justify full replacement. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars, while a full replacement costs more but resets the clock for decades. We show you the condition of the pipe we expose so you can make the call with real information.

    Main Water Line Repair in Fayetteville

    The main water line brings water from the municipal supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause serious water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure. We use advanced detection methods to find the exact location of the problem, limiting excavation and disruption to your property in Fayetteville. We also check for common issues like root intrusion or shifting soil.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Fayetteville

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are experienced in repairing both. PEX offers flexibility and corrosion resistance, while copper is known for its durability. We carry a full stock of matching fittings and tools for each material, ensuring a proper, code-compliant repair. Our experience extends to identifying common failure points for each type, such as fitting issues with PEX or pinhole leaks in older copper lines, frequent in homes with harder water.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Fayetteville

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a particularly challenging issue. Older homes in Arkansas often still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, both of which corrode from the inside with age. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to serious structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to locate slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak quickly in Fayetteville is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.
