    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Fayetteville

    A standard 40 to 50 gallon tank usually costs $1,600 to $3,500 installed. Tankless jobs run about $3,000 to $6,500 depending on gas line and vent needs.

    It looks simple but involves gas, electrical and plumbing connections, plus a vent that has to carry exhaust out of the house every time the burner fires. Wrong installs cause leaks, carbon monoxide or electrical hazards. A licensed plumber is the safe call.

    Household size and peak demand decide the size. Most homes with two to four people use a 40 to 50 gallon tank, and larger households move up to 60 to 80 gallons. Tankless goes by flow rate.

    Manufacturer warranties on the tank and parts usually run anywhere from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model and the brand you choose. DGR Plumbing backs the installation labor too. We review terms with you.

    Doing it yourself means handling gas, 240-volt power and pressurized water at once. Mistakes can cause gas leaks, CO exposure or electrical fires, and a DIY install can void the warranty. We do not recommend it.

    Tank heaters usually last 10 to 15 years, and tankless units can go 20 or more. Hard water and skipped maintenance shorten that, while yearly flushing and anode checks stretch it a good deal past the average. Flushing helps most.

    We install Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and a few other brands with good parts support and long tank warranties. We recommend based on price and use.
