    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    While less common than traditional softeners, no-salt water conditioners also require maintenance and occasional repair.     Control Valve Repair in Fayetteville

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, directing the flow of water and starting regeneration cycles. When it malfunctions, your system can stop softening water or waste salt. DGR Plumbing technicians are experts at diagnosing and repairing common control valve issues, whether mechanical or electronic, ensuring precise timing and efficient operation. We stock common replacement parts to speed your repair in Fayetteville.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms in the brine tank and keeps salt from dissolving. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. We also suggest salt types that bridge less in warm or humid spaces.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Fayetteville

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use.
