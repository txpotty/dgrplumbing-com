    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Fayetteville

    Our routine tune-ups involve a thorough inspection of your entire water softener system. We check for proper salt levels, inspect the brine tank for salt bridges and confirm the resin bed is working effectively. This preventive service is vital wherever hard water minerals can quickly affect system performance if not regularly addressed.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Fayetteville

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs much less than a new system.

    Water Softener Brand Servicing in Fayetteville

    DGR Plumbing has extensive experience maintaining all major water softener brands, including Culligan, Kenmore, Whirlpool, Fleck, Kinetico and more. Our technicians are familiar with the specific components and maintenance requirements of each manufacturer.     Control Valve and Injector Service in Fayetteville, AR

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Fayetteville, AR

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their capacity. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We confirm your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Fayetteville, AR

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can build up, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening. This is a common issue we address in Fayetteville homes.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Fayetteville

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, preventing the need for a costly full system replacement in Fayetteville.
