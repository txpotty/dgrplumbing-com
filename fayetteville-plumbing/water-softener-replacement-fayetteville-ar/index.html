    Brine Tank Replacement in Fayetteville

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the right salt type for your system.

    Softener Loop Installation in Fayetteville, AR

    Many homes have a softener loop near the main water line that makes for easy installation. When a home lacks one, we can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. We also route a drain line to a laundry box, floor drain or approved location. A good loop keeps outdoor hose bibs on hard water to save salt. This makes future service simpler.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Fayetteville, AR

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Fayetteville

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Fayetteville

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That determines the resin capacity needed.

    Most residential water softener replacements can be finished within 3 to 6 hours, assuming no major plumbing changes are required. Our skilled plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption, restoring soft water to your home on the same day.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can look into other causes.

    In many areas, a plumbing permit is generally not required for a direct like-for-like water softener replacement if no significant changes to the existing plumbing are made. However, if new water lines or drain lines are being installed or relocated, a permit may be required. We will advise you on any permitting requirements.

    New water softeners usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. DGR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Exact warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and foul aerators and water heaters. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, lower efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is the best defense for combating these issues in Fayetteville homes.

    With proper maintenance, most quality water softeners can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. Factors like water hardness, usage volume and the quality of the original installation can affect its lifespan. Regular servicing helps extend the life of your unit.
