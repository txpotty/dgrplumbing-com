    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Fayetteville

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles.     Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Fayetteville, AR

    Basement Wall Sealing in Fayetteville, AR

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Humidity speeds corrosion on older metal pipe, so galvanized lines in Arkansas homes often develop leaks and weak water pressure. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Fayetteville, AR

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Houses built from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s may contain polybutylene supply lines, which are prone to fail without warning. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with interior drain tile.     Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Fayetteville
