    The duration of a septic tank repair depends on the complexity of the issue. Simple clog removals might take a few hours, while more extensive repairs like drain field work could span several days. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    Common causes of septic tank failure include improper waste disposal, such as flushing non-biodegradable items, lack of regular pumping, tree root intrusion, heavy vehicle traffic over the drain field and poor soil drainage. Septic systems are very common across rural Arkansas, and many homes outside city limits rely on them for all wastewater.

    Typical signs include sewage odors around the tank or drain field, slow drains or backed-up toilets, lush green patches of grass over the drain field, gurgling sounds from pipes and standing water in your yard. If you notice any of these, call DGR Plumbing right away.

    Many households need pumping every three to five years, depending on tank size and household use. Regular pumping protects the leach field from solids.
