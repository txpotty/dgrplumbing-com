    Septic Code and Permit Work in Fayetteville, AR

    New tanks and new fields need a permit from the local health authority in most of the country, along with any change to the system's layout. We draw the site plan and file the paperwork, then schedule the inspection before the trench is backfilled. Small fixes like a tee usually skip the permit. The inspector's sign-off goes in your file.

    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Fayetteville

    A slow toilet and a gurgle in the tub usually point to a clog between the house and the tank. We run a camera down the line first, then clear it with a jetter set up for a 4-inch building sewer. Grease and wipes cause most of these, and what we do next depends on what comes out of the pipe. Roots mean a joint has opened.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Fayetteville, AR

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Camera Inspection of Septic Baffles in Fayetteville, AR

    Some tanks have no riser, so we run a push camera through the small inspection port to see the baffles. That saves digging up the whole lid. If the outlet tee is cracked or missing, we dig at that end only, then install a PVC tee with a filter housing. Concrete lids get a riser while they are open.

    Drain Field Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Fayetteville

    Sewage in the shower pan on a Sunday is an emergency, and we treat it like one. The first thing we ask is whether the tank has a riser we can reach, because uncovering a buried lid can add an hour or more. Stop using water until the tech arrives. Then we find the blockage, clear it and tell you what caused it.

    Septic Repair for Businesses in Fayetteville, AR

    Daycares and small shops on septic put more flow through the tank, more often, than a typical three-bedroom house with four people in it. Kitchen grease and restroom paper towels cause most of the trouble. We add a larger effluent filter and set a pump-out schedule based on use. Repairs get scheduled around your hours.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Fayetteville
