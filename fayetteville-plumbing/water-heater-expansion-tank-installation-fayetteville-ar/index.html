    Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Fayetteville

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are widespread. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you steady pressure throughout the house.

    Brands and Tank Materials in Fayetteville, AR

    We install potable-rated thermal expansion tanks from brands such as Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Potable tanks use a butyl diaphragm and a lined interior so water never touches bare steel. Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see more mineral-heavy water from groundwater sources. Stainless steel connections resist corrosion from hard or aggressive water. We avoid tanks designed only for hydronic heating, since they are not approved for drinking water.

    Preventing Water Heater Damage in Fayetteville

    Thermal expansion is a leading cause of premature water heater failure in Fayetteville homes. Without an expansion tank, constant pressure swings stress the water heater's tank, connections and internal components, leading to leaks, reduced efficiency and a shorter lifespan. Our installation service directly addresses this issue, providing a buffer that protects your investment.     Expansion Tank Replacement in Fayetteville, AR

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.
