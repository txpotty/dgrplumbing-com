    Thermal Expansion Tank Installation in Fayetteville

    Many water utilities install pressure regulators or backflow devices that create a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure spikes can stress your tank and fixtures. Code commonly requires a thermal expansion tank on these systems. We install a properly sized expansion tank and pre-charge it to match your water pressure. This simple add-on can help your new heater last longer.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Fayetteville

    Power vent gas water heaters use an electric blower to push exhaust through PVC pipe, which lets them sit far from a chimney or roof flue. Venting length and number of elbows must stay within the manufacturer's limits. Power vent tanks cost more but solve tricky placement problems.
