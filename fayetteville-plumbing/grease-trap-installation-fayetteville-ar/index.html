    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Fayetteville

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water. They are common in small cafes, coffee shops and quick-service restaurants with tight space. We install units from Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura sized by gallons per minute. Each install includes a flow control fitting and proper venting to keep the trap working as designed. These units need frequent cleaning, so we place them where staff can reach the lid easily.

    The cost of grease trap installation in Fayetteville, AR varies significantly based on the size, type, and complexity of the system.     Installation time depends on the size and complexity of the grease trap.     Grease trap installation is a complex commercial plumbing task that requires specialized knowledge, tools, and adherence to strict local codes.     The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10 to 15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20 to 30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to lengthening its lifespan.
