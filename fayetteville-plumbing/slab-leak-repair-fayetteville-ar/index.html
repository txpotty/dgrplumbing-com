    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Fayetteville

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks promptly helps protect your foundation.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Fayetteville

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes end future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Fayetteville

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is badly deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Fayetteville with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Fayetteville, AR

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to limit wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Whether your home in Fayetteville, AR has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both.
