    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Fayetteville

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Fayetteville, AR

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Fayetteville

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Fayetteville, AR

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. It adds up quickly. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Fayetteville, AR

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Fayetteville

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Fayetteville

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way. If the drywall is already off, blocking gets added between studs so a grab bar can screw into wood, not hollow-wall anchors. We also center the bowl to ADA spacing.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Fayetteville

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.
