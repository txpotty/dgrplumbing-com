    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Fayetteville

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. We shut off every appliance and watch the meter's test dial, then close branch valves one at a time to find which section is leaking.     Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Fayetteville

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age.     Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Fayetteville, AR

Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.
