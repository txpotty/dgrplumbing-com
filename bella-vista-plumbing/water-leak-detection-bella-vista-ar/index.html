    Slab Leak Detection in Bella Vista

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. DGR Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Bella Vista, AR

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Hot, humid summers dominate the plumbing picture in Arkansas, with crawlspace moisture and sweating cold lines causing quiet damage.

    Pool Water Leak Detection in Bella Vista, AR

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak protects the pool and the soil around it.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Bella Vista

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. DGR Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Irrigation Leak Detection in Bella Vista

    Drip and sprinkler systems run underground across yards and often account for hidden water loss. Cracked tubing, broken emitters and stuck valves can waste large amounts of water. We isolate the irrigation system, test each zone and trace leaking lines. Repairs restore proper watering for your landscape.
