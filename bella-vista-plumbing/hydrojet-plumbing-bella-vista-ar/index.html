    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Bella Vista

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Bella Vista

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Bella Vista

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Homes with root-prone sewers usually jet every year or two, and we keep a camera record from each visit so you can see whether the roots are coming back slower, faster or the same.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Bella Vista

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Bella Vista, AR

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Bella Vista, AR

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Bella Vista, AR

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Bella Vista

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.
