    Tankless vs. Tank Water Heater Conversion in Bella Vista, AR

    Considering converting from a traditional tank water heater to a tankless system in your Bella Vista, AR home? This is a common and worthwhile upgrade, but it requires specialized expertise. We handle the entire conversion, which can involve rerouting gas lines, upgrading electrical service, modifying water lines and installing new venting.     Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Bella Vista

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or seized. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Bella Vista

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Big temperature swings between seasons expand and contract pipes and fittings, which can loosen aging joints over time. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth it.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Bella Vista

    Restaurants, salons and small offices often use multiple tankless units linked together on a rack system. When one or more units fail, we replace them and rebalance the cascade controls so they share the load. We check gas pressure under full demand, since commercial racks can draw more than 600,000 BTU combined. Scheduling is flexible, so we can work after hours and keep your business open. We also set up a descaling schedule suited to your water usage.
