    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Bella Vista, AR

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. If the sinks have good hot pressure and the shower does not, the cartridge is the first suspect.

    Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Bella Vista

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot. Replacing the cartridge solves it. Water that never gets hot enough may only need the limit stop turned.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Bella Vista, AR

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. We stock both. Original owners can often get the cartridge free under warranty.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Bella Vista

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Emergency Shower Leak Repair in Bella Vista

    Water coming through a ceiling below a bathroom needs fast action. Shut the main valve, catch the drip in a bucket and keep people out from under any sagging drywall, since a soaked ceiling can let go without warning. Then call us. We find the source, stop it and dry out what we can that day.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Bella Vista

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. We clear and test the drain. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Bella Vista, AR

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Bella Vista, AR

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. If the cartridge is clogged too, we replace it and test the flow again.
