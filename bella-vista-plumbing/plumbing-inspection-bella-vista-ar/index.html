    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Bella Vista

    A standard plumbing inspection in Bella Vista, AR usually costs $150 to $350. Size changes the price. The base price covers pipe, fixtures, the heater, drains and pressure, and the sewer camera is usually a separate line since it adds time and equipment. You get the price when you book.

    Our full inspection covers supply pipe, drains, fixtures, shutoff valves, the water heater, gas connectors and the sewer line. Pressure gets measured. A meter leak test, a check for slab leak signs and hard water damage and a look at any softener or filter are also included.

    For an older home, it usually pays for itself. Old pipe hides problems. Homes with galvanized supply pipe, clay or cast iron sewers or original water heaters often have problems that are invisible from the outside, and an inspection lets you plan repairs instead of reacting to a failure.

    A general home inspector checks basic function, while a plumber goes deeper with sewer cameras, pressure tests and pipe identification. Many buyers hire both.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Noisy drains are a clue. A damp wall, a musty cabinet or a warm spot on the floor can mean a hidden leak, and an inspection finds it before it spreads.

    Watching your water meter, looking under sinks and checking for leaks are all things you can do yourself. A professional inspection adds camera video, pressure testing and expert analysis.

    Our plumber explains each issue, how serious it is and what the repair options are. The choice is yours. We can quote any repairs on the spot or later, and you can have us do the work, use another plumber or hand the report to the seller during negotiations.
