    Undermount Sink Installation in Bella Vista

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Bella Vista, AR

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Bella Vista

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Bella Vista, AR

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Bella Vista, AR
