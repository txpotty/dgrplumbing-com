    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Bella Vista, AR

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. What they do well is keep scale off pipe walls and heater elements. No salt, no drain. We can replace one or switch you to a true softener.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Bella Vista

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. A tank water heater on hard water can lose a noticeable share of its efficiency as scale builds on the bottom, and tankless units scale up even faster. Soft water prevents it.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Bella Vista, AR

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Bella Vista, AR

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Bella Vista, AR

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.
