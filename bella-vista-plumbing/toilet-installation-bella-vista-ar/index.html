    Toilet Brands We Install in Bella Vista

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Each one has its own parts quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Bella Vista

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Bella Vista, AR

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. The floor underneath stays open. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Bella Vista

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    Toilet Replacement and Upgrades in Bella Vista, AR

    Swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet for a modern one is the easiest water savings in the house. The old unit comes out in about twenty minutes. Most of the remaining time goes into cleaning the flange and setting the new bowl on shims so it does not rock on a floor that has settled over the years. We haul the old one away.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Bella Vista, AR

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Bella Vista

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Bella Vista, AR

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.
