    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. Should those stops be stuck, the house main shutoff is the backup. We come fast for these.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Bella Vista

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Bella Vista
