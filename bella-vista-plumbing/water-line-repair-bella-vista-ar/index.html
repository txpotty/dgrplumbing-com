    Copper Water Line Repair in Bella Vista

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, then we look over the pipe on either side for more pitting before we backfill.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Bella Vista, AR

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. DGR Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. We can usually stop it within the hour. If the repair will run long, we rig a temporary bypass so the house has water while we work.

    Main Water Line Leak Repair in Bella Vista

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. Confirming it takes about ten minutes. The house shutoff gets closed, the dial gets watched, and continued movement tells us the leak is in the buried line. Ground microphones find the spot. A repair coupling or a new length of pipe goes in, and then we pressure test it.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Bella Vista

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Bella Vista, AR

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and clog aerators and water heaters. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Bella Vista

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. Then the new pipe gets pulled through underground. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Bella Vista, AR

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. No water means no open sign.
