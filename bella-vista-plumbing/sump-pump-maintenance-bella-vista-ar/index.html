    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Bella Vista, AR

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get stuck.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Bella Vista, AR

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Bella Vista, AR

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements.     Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Bella Vista
