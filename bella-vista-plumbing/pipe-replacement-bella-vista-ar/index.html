    Copper Pipe Replacement in Bella Vista, AR

    Copper still has fans. It is durable and heat tolerant. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Bella Vista, AR

    Galvanized steel was common before the 1960s. Inside, rust builds once the coating fails. Over decades the opening shrinks, pressure drops and the water turns brown after sitting, while threaded joints corrode through first. Replacing it is the only fix.

    PEX Repiping in Bella Vista

    PEX repipes go faster than copper. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Bella Vista

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. It resists scale and corrosion. Homeowners near Bella Vista on well water benefit from periodic testing and the right filter to protect pipes and appliances. We use PEX-a with expansion fittings or PEX-b with crimp rings depending on the job, and we run it in home runs from a manifold or in trunk-and-branch layouts. Fewer joints, fewer leaks.
