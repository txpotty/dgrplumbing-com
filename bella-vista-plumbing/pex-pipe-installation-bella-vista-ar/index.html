    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Bella Vista

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings.     PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Bella Vista, AR

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory.
