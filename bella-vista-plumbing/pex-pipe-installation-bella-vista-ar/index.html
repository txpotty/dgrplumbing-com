    Why Choose DGR Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Bella Vista, AR?

    PEX piping offers superior freeze resistance than copper or CPVC. Hard freezes hit Arkansas most winters, especially in the northern and higher areas, and exposed pipes can split during a sharp cold snap. We make sure your new PEX system is installed correctly to get the most from that benefit.

    • Flexibility, Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and connections, cutting down potential leak points in your plumbing system.
    • Freeze Resistant, PEX can expand and contract, making it more resistant to bursting in freezing temperatures than rigid pipes.
    • Corrosion Resistant, Unlike metal pipes, PEX does not corrode or develop pinhole leaks from hard water.
