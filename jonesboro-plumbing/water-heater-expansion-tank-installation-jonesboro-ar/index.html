    The cost for water heater expansion tank installation in Jonesboro generally ranges from $250 to $500, including parts and labor.     A standalone expansion tank generally does not require a permit, but one is often included when it is installed with a new water heater. We follow local requirements on every job.

    Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior or a water heater that frequently cycles on and off. You might also notice fluctuating water pressure or a banging sound in your pipes, which points to excessive pressure buildup.

    An expansion tank is necessary in closed plumbing systems to absorb the increased water volume and pressure that occur when water is heated. Without it, thermal expansion can damage your water heater, pipes and fixtures, leading to leaks and premature system failure. Many local codes in Arkansas call for them.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve, check valve or backflow preventer on the main, you likely have a closed system that needs one. Many local codes require it whenever a water heater is installed on a closed system.

    A waterlogged tank feels heavy and sounds solid when tapped near the top. Water coming out of the air valve when pressed is a sure sign the bladder has failed.

    While less common than with traditional tank water heaters, an expansion tank can still be required or beneficial for tankless water heaters, especially if your home has a closed plumbing system because of a backflow preventer or pressure reducing valve. We will evaluate your setup to provide the best recommendation.
