    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Jonesboro, AR

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. They need no drain line. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Jonesboro

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Most people notice less salt in a few weeks.
