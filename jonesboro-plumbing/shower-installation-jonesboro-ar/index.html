    Shower Stall Installation in Jonesboro

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. Fiberglass and acrylic bases go down on a mortar bed so they cannot flex, and flex is the usual reason a base cracks early. Then the drain goes in. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Jonesboro

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. We check the heater. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Jonesboro

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. The enclosure needs a sealed door and a sloped ceiling.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Jonesboro

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support.     Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.
