    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Jonesboro

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it.     Gas Water Heater Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. That makes placement easier.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Jonesboro

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Permits and inspections are commonly required for water heaters, new fixtures, gas lines, and sewer work, with rules applied across the state. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Jonesboro

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. We follow them exactly. That protects your coverage.
