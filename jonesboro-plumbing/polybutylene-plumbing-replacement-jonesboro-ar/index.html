    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Jonesboro

    The cost for polybutylene plumbing replacement in Jonesboro can range from $4,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on the size of your home, the chosen material (PEX is generally less expensive than copper), and the complexity of the installation, especially with slab foundations.     Look for gray, flexible plastic pipe at your water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff, often stamped PB2110. Homes built from the late 1970s to mid 1990s are most apt to have it.

    Removing and replacing an entire supply system requires routing, pressure testing and permits. A licensed crew finishes faster and provides warranties and paperwork.

    Properly installed PEX piping typically has a lifespan of 25 to 50 years, while copper piping can last 50 to 70 years or even longer. Both are major upgrades from polybutylene and offer decades of reliable service for your Jonesboro, AR home.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A and Type L copper with Viega ProPress or soldered fittings. We recommend materials based on your home and water.

    Most policies do not pay for replacing old pipe, though they may cover sudden water damage. Some insurers raise rates or decline coverage for homes with polybutylene.
