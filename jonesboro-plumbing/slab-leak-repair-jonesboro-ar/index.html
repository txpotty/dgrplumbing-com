    Slab Leak Detection in Jonesboro

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking.     Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Jonesboro, AR

    Whether your home in Jonesboro, AR has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are proficient in repairing both.     Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Jonesboro, AR

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Arkansas plumbing codes and the slab is properly restored, preventing future issues. We take great care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Jonesboro home.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Jonesboro, AR

    When the leak is isolated and the rest of the pipe is in good shape, a spot repair through the slab is a practical option. We cut a small opening, excavate to the pipe and replace the damaged section. The trench is compacted and the concrete is patched. Flooring is then restored by your preferred contractor.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Jonesboro, AR

    A major slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Prompt action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Jonesboro

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Jonesboro with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Jonesboro, AR

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a good choice for many homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Jonesboro home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.
