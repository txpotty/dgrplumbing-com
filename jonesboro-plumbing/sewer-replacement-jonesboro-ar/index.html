    Addressing Tree Root Intrusions in Jonesboro, AR

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line damage, especially with older, more porous pipe materials like clay or cast iron. Roots can penetrate tiny cracks, grow inside the pipe, and eventually cause blockages or even burst the line. Our sewer replacement services specifically address root-damaged pipes, often recommending more resistant materials like PVC or ABS to prevent future intrusions. We also advise on tree placement to protect your new sewer system.

    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Jonesboro

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage.     Sewer Pipe Material Upgrades in Jonesboro, AR

    Many older homes in Jonesboro, AR still have original sewer lines made of clay, cast iron, or Orangeburg pipe, which are prone to degradation, cracking, and root intrusion over time. When we perform a sewer replacement, we often recommend upgrading to modern, durable materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) or ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene). These materials are more flexible, resistant to corrosion and roots, and have a much longer lifespan, providing a significant upgrade to your home's plumbing infrastructure.
