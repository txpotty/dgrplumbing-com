    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Jonesboro

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    The basin is where the system lives, and a 30-gallon size is common for a single basement bathroom. Bigger basins let the pump run longer, fewer times a day, which is easier on the motor and switch, so we size up when the pit serves a laundry or several fixtures. Every lid gets sealed.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for decades, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. They work well for new systems and for drop-in replacements, since many Zoeller models fit existing pits and discharge piping as is. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Jonesboro

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Severe storms and heavy spring rain are common in Arkansas, and saturated ground can overwhelm sump pumps and flood sewer lines. Backup power can help.

    Commercial Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Jonesboro, AR

    Businesses in Jonesboro, AR with restrooms, kitchens or break rooms below grade need ejector systems that keep up with heavier, steadier use than a house. A duplex system with an alternating panel is common, so if one pump fails the other keeps things running. Downtime costs money. We size both pumps to carry the full load.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Jonesboro

    Residential ejector systems in Jonesboro serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. Each system needs a sealed basin, a pump with a float, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Jonesboro, AR

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Filters save impellers.

    Zoeller Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Jonesboro

    Zoeller's sewage line covers home and heavier-duty use, in cast iron with tethered or vertical float switches. We pick the model by horsepower and pump curve for your lift, and a typical basement bathroom lands on a four-tenths or half horsepower unit with two-inch solids handling. Bigger lifts need more.
