    What Our Tankless Maintenance Covers in Jonesboro, AR

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Jonesboro, AR

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.

    Noritz Descaling in Jonesboro, AR

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. While the cycle runs, we check the fan motor and exhaust path.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Jonesboro

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. The pump pulls solution from a bucket, through the cold port, out the hot port and back again. After the cycle we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Jonesboro

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. The trap gets cleaned and the media checked. Spent media gets replaced. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Jonesboro, AR

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. The flue gets a look for obstructions. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.

    What Tankless Maintenance Saves in Jonesboro

    Scale on an exchanger acts like insulation. A unit with a thick layer burns noticeably more gas for the same shower than a clean one, and the extra cost adds up month after month. We lay out the maintenance price next to the likely repair cost so you can decide.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Jonesboro, AR

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle.     Tankless Vent Inspection in Jonesboro

On a condensing unit the PVC or polypropylene vent should pitch back toward the heater. We check every joint and test for carbon monoxide near the unit. Wasp nests in vent caps are common.
