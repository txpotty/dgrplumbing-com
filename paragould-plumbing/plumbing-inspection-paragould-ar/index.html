    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Paragould, AR

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Some need replacing soon. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Paragould

    Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and foul aerators and water heaters. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Paragould, AR

    Big temperature swings between seasons expand and contract pipes and fittings, which can loosen older joints over time. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.
