    To prevent pipes from freezing in Paragould, insulate all exposed pipes, especially those in unheated areas like crawl spaces, attics, and basements. Disconnect garden hoses, drain outdoor spigots, and consider keeping a small trickle of water running from faucets during extreme cold snaps. Sealing air leaks near pipes can also help.

    A faucet that only trickles or does not run after a cold night is the most typical sign. You may also see frost on exposed pipe or hear odd noises in the walls. If one fixture works and another does not, the frozen section is typically on the line to the dead fixture.

    You can open the faucet and carefully warm an exposed pipe with a hair dryer. Never use a torch or open flame. If the pipe is inside a wall or you cannot find the frozen section, call a plumber.

    While any pipe can freeze, older galvanized steel or copper pipes in uninsulated areas are often more susceptible to bursting due to their rigid material.
