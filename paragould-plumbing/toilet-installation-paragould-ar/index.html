    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Paragould, AR

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first.     Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Paragould

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Paragould

    Labor for a standard toilet installation in Paragould typically falls between $250 and $600, not counting the toilet itself. Flange repairs add to that. Extra work such as a rotted flange or soft subfloor raises the price, and we quote it before we begin.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the toilet drain. Twelve inches is the most common. You will also see 10 and 14 inch rough-ins, and buying the wrong one means the toilet will not fit or sits well off the wall. Measure from the wall itself, not the baseboard.

    Homeowners set basic toilets all the time. Flange height and bolt torque are where it goes wrong. A flange that sits below the finished floor needs an extender or a thicker seal, and a nut tightened one turn too far can crack the base, so on new floors and wall-hung toilets a pro install is the safer bet.

    A one-piece toilet is cast as a single unit, so there is no tank-to-bowl joint to leak. It costs more and weighs more. A two-piece bolts the tank to the bowl with a rubber gasket, which makes it lighter to carry up stairs and cheaper to fix, since you can replace just the tank or just the bowl if one cracks. Both flush well.

    Two warranties apply. The manufacturer covers the toilet. Most brands give one to five years on parts like the fill valve and flapper and a longer term, sometimes lifetime, on the vitreous china, while our own guarantee covers any leak traced back to how we set it.

    Lower water bills come first. Pre-1994 toilets can use nearly three times what a 1.28 gallon model does. Newer bowls also have wider glazed trapways that clog less, and the smoother porcelain stays cleaner between scrubbings than an old pitted bowl ever did.

    Just about any brand you can buy at a supply house or home center. We see a lot of TOTO and American Standard. Landlords like Gerber and Mansfield for cheap, easy parts, and a quick call is usually enough for us to point you to a model. Bring us the model number.
