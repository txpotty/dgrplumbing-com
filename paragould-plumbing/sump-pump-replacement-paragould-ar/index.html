    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Paragould

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Paragould, AR usually ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, submersible or pedestal, its horsepower and any needed pit changes.     We install a variety of proven sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you pick the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are important, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    While it may seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and making sure the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Paragould codes.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years. We also offer a labor warranty.

    Yes, in addition to replacement, we offer sump pump maintenance services to keep your system ready to run. Regular maintenance, including cleaning the pit, checking the float switch and inspecting the discharge line, can extend the life of your pump and prevent surprise failures.     Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or major discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.
