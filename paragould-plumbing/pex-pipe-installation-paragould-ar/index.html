    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Paragould

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Paragould

    Pinhole leaks in copper rarely show up alone. Water chemistry, high flow velocity and contact with soil under a slab can all pit the pipe wall from the inside, and once a few pinholes appear, more are usually on the way. Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see harder water from groundwater sources. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. We use listed transition fittings anywhere PEX ties into copper that stays.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Paragould, AR

    Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel is the cheapest time to replace old supply lines. With the studs bare, we can run PEX back to a manifold or to the nearest good trunk, set new stub-outs for the sink and dishwasher, and add a line for the fridge ice maker. An addition gets sized on its own. A far-end bathroom may need a three-quarter inch feed to hold pressure.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Paragould, AR

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Paragould, AR

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool. The fitting goes in while the plastic is still expanded, and the material's memory pulls it back down around the barb, sealing harder over the first minute. Cold weather slows it. We let joints set before testing.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in Paragould, AR

    Price on a repipe comes down to a few things: bathroom count, square footage, how many stories, and how easy the attic and walls are to work in. PEX almost always comes in below copper because the material is cheaper and a crew can run a house in two to four days instead of a week. Patching is sometimes quoted on its own line. We price PEX-A, PEX-B and copper side by side when you ask.
