    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Paragould, AR

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Paragould

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an accurate temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Big temperature swings between seasons expand and contract pipes and fittings, which can loosen aging joints over time. Properly working components are vital for both efficiency and safety.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Paragould, AR

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Paragould, AR use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently.     Tank Water Heater Flushing in Paragould

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clear. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Paragould

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Paragould, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing expensive tank leaks.
