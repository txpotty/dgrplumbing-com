    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Replacement in Paragould

    Faucet replacement labor typically runs $150 to $350. Including a quality mid-range faucet, most jobs total $300 to $750. Touchless or high-end faucets can bring the total to $800 or more.

    New faucets typically carry manufacturer warranties, often lifetime on cartridges for major brands. We also guarantee our installation workmanship.

    Homeowners near Paragould on well water benefit from routine testing and the right filter to protect pipes and appliances. Hard water can lead to mineral deposits, or limescale, building up inside faucets. This buildup can reduce water flow, cause drips, and corrode internal components over time. Regular maintenance or choosing faucets designed for hard water can help them last longer.

    We service and install all major faucet brands, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and many others. Our plumbers are well versed in the specific installation requirements and common issues for a wide variety of manufacturers.

    We check your sink's hole count and spacing and recommend matching faucets. Many single-hole faucets include a deck plate for three-hole sinks.

    Typically, a permit is not required for a direct faucet replacement unless it involves significant changes to the existing plumbing system or relocating water lines. If there are major changes, we will advise you on any necessary permitting.

    Faucets with ceramic disc cartridges from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Grohe tend to last well. Keeping aerators clean and using a water softener also help.

    Most standard faucet replacements can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new supply lines, drilling extra holes, or dealing with heavily corroded pipes, might take longer, but we always strive for efficiency.
