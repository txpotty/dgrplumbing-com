    New Expansion Tank Installation in Paragould

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays stable.

    While tankless water heaters do not store hot water, an expansion tank can still be beneficial, and sometimes required, in Paragould, AR plumbing systems. If your tankless unit is part of a closed system, an expansion tank can help absorb pressure spikes from the cold water supply or reduce wear on components.     Tank Sizing and Placement in Paragould

    Correct sizing and placement are key to an effective water heater expansion tank in Paragould.     Pressure Reducing Valve and Expansion Tank Pairing in Paragould

    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are common. The PRV protects your fixtures but also turns your plumbing into a closed system, which makes an expansion tank necessary. We test the PRV output and replace it if it is creeping or failing. Pairing a healthy PRV with a correctly charged expansion tank gives you steady pressure throughout the house.

    Expansion Tank Replacement in Paragould, AR

    Expansion tanks wear out over time as the internal bladder or diaphragm fails and the tank fills with water. A waterlogged tank feels heavy, and tapping it produces a dull sound all the way up instead of a hollow top. Pressing the air valve and getting water instead of air is a clear sign it has failed. We replace failed tanks with a correctly sized new unit and check the precharge before installation.
