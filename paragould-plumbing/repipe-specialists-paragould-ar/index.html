    Efficient PEX Repiping in Paragould, AR

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Paragould, AR. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Copper Repiping in Paragould, AR

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes usually cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Paragould

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Paragould. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.
