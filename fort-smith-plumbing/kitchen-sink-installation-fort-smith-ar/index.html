    Apron-Front Sinks in Fort Smith, AR

    Apron sinks need a cabinet cut to fit the front panel, and the bowl must be set before the counter gets measured. We set the sink and frame early, so the fabricator measures from the actual bowl instead of a spec sheet. It prevents a crooked reveal.     Same-Day Sink Replacement in Fort Smith, AR

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out needs replacing, not patching. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, which makes same-day replacement common, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. We swap the old stops as well.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Fort Smith, AR

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Composite Sink Installation in Fort Smith

    Granite and quartz composite sinks are tough on top but can chip at the drain opening if a strainer is overtightened. Hand tight comes first. Most composite brands want silicone rather than putty under the strainer because putty can stain them, so we go by the maker's instructions.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Fort Smith

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Fort Smith

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs.
