    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Fort Smith, AR

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Pre-Purchase Inspection With Sewer Video in Fort Smith

    Plumbing problems are some of the most expensive surprises after closing. We check supply pipe, record the sewer, date the water heater and test every fixture. Your agent gets the report. Buyers often use the findings to request repairs or credits, and the sewer video by itself can head off a very costly surprise.

    Water Heater Inspections in Fort Smith

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. We check tankless units for scale. Gas heaters get a look at the flame, the drip leg and the vent draft, since a poorly venting heater is a safety issue.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Fort Smith, AR

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Fort Smith

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. Every unit gets its own notes. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Fort Smith, AR

    Commercial buildings have equipment homes do not, so our commercial inspections cover more ground. We look at backflow assemblies, grease interceptors, commercial water heaters, flush valves and the building sewer. Inspectors like documentation. The written report helps facility managers plan and budget repairs before a health inspector or the water provider writes them up.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Fort Smith, AR

    Older homes in Fort Smith often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout. We mark it on the ground. With depth and position marked, repair pricing gets accurate, and choosing between a spot fix, a liner or replacement gets easier.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Fort Smith, AR

    Humidity speeds corrosion on older metal pipe, so galvanized lines in Arkansas homes often develop leaks and low water pressure. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. We look at pipe insulation. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.
