    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in Fort Smith

    The cost for a gas water heater installation in Fort Smith usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,000, depending on the unit size, type (tank vs. tankless) and any needed gas line or venting modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after a site assessment.

    Choosing the right size depends on your household's peak hot water demand, determined by the number of residents and hot water fixtures. Our technicians will assess your needs during the consultation to recommend the appropriate gallon capacity or flow rate for your Fort Smith home.

    We install all major reputable brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State Water Heaters and Navien for tankless models. We can help you choose the best brand and model for your specific needs and budget.

    New gas water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. DGR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, giving you peace of mind.

    A conventional gas water heater stores and heats a large volume of water in a tank, providing a limited supply. A gas tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit, offering an endless supply but requiring more complex venting and gas line work.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced annually. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency and catch potential issues before they become major problems, supporting safe operation.

    Rusty water, leaks, pilot problems and slow recovery are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement may be the smarter option.

    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is typically supplied by a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and confirm correct setup for your fuel source.
