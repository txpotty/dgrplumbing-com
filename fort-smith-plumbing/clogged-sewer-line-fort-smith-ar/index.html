    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Fort Smith, AR

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Fort Smith, AR. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are usually isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at pinpointing the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Fort Smith

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another backup.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Fort Smith, AR

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an excellent preventative maintenance tool and is particularly effective for older pipes in Fort Smith that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Fort Smith, AR

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can decide.
