    Slab Leak Reroutes in Fort Smith

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile.     Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Fort Smith, AR

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, so we isolate the cold side, map the pipe with a line tracer and listen along the route with a ground microphone.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Fort Smith

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Fort Smith, AR home.

    Non-Invasive Slab Leak Locating in Fort Smith, AR

    We touch the floor last. Pressure tests, line tracing, thermal scans and acoustic listening all happen from the surface in your Fort Smith, AR home, without drilling or cutting anything. Once two tools agree, we mark the spot and go over repairs, some of which avoid the slab entirely.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Fort Smith, AR

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Fort Smith, AR home.
