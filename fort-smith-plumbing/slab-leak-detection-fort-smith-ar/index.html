    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Fort Smith, AR

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Fort Smith

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Fort Smith, AR

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can typically complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Water and Sewer Line Slab Leaks in Fort Smith, AR

    Slab leaks can affect both freshwater supply lines and sewer drain pipes. Our detection services are equipped to identify both types of leaks, which present different challenges. Sewer line leaks often involve foul odors and slow drainage, while water line leaks can appear as wet spots or high water bills in your Fort Smith, AR home. We have specific methods for each.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Fort Smith

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is a good fit when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Leak Detection for Older Arkansas Homes in Fort Smith, AR

    Many older homes in Fort Smith, AR have copper or galvanized steel pipes that are more susceptible to corrosion and leaks over time. Our technicians are highly experienced in detecting leaks in these older systems, understanding their common failure points. We provide expert analysis for the challenges presented by vintage plumbing under concrete slabs.
