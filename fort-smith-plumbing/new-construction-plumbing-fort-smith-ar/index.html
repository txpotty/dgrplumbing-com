    Water Heater and Recirculation Installation in Fort Smith, AR

    New homes often use a tank heater in the garage or utility room, or a tankless unit on an exterior wall. Rural wells in Arkansas often carry iron, sediment, or a sulfur odor, which can stain fixtures and clog aerators and water heaters. On tankless units we add isolation valves so the heat exchanger can be flushed without cutting pipe. A recirculation loop is worth considering.     Rough-In Plumbing Services in Fort Smith

    Top-out is the second half of rough-in, after framing. Supply lines, drains and vents go through the framing and up to the roof, with stub-outs set to the cabinet drawings. Testing comes next.     Water Heater Installation for New Builds in Fort Smith

    Picking the water heater during design saves a gas line reroute later. A 50-gallon tank covers most three-bedroom homes. Tankless units suit bigger houses but need a larger gas line and their own vent route, both planned at rough-in. We set expansion tanks and pans where code calls for them.

    Water Heater Setup for New Builds in Fort Smith

    Tank or tankless, the venting decides the install location. Atmospheric tanks need a metal flue straight up, and power-vent or condensing units can go out a sidewall in PVC. We set the heater on a stand in a garage where code requires it. The T&P discharge runs to an approved termination.
