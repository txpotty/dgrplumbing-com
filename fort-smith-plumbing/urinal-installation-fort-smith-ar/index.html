    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Fort Smith

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible.     Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Fort Smith

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. A hairline crack in vitreous china will eventually leak, and a bowl stained by years of hard scale often cannot be cleaned back to white, so a new fixture on the old carrier is usually the fastest fix. Carrier bolts get inspected first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Fort Smith

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. A cartridge or sealing liquid blocks sewer gas while letting urine through, and it must be replaced on schedule. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.
