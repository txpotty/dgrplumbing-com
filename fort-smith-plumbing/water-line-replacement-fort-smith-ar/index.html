    Copper Water Line Installation in Fort Smith

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it.     PEX Water Line Installation in Fort Smith, AR

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Crawlspace pipes, exterior wall lines, and outdoor faucets are the usual freeze failures, so insulation and vent covers make a real difference. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Fort Smith

    We buy PEX in long coils. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.
