    Expansion Tank Installation in Fort Smith, AR

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Fort Smith

    The cost of water heater installation in Fort Smith, AR can range from $1,200 to $4,000 or more, depending on the type of unit (traditional tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical upgrades. Tankless units usually have higher installation costs due to more complex venting and utility requirements. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. DGR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more economical choice. Newer models also offer much better energy efficiency.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry serious risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes often require a licensed plumber for this work.

    DGR Plumbing installs all major trusted brands of water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz. We work with both gas and electric models, as well as many sizes and efficiencies. Our team can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your hot water demands and budget.

    A standard tank replacement usually takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    In most areas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement or installation to ensure the work meets local building and safety codes. Permits and inspections are commonly required for water heaters, new fixtures, gas lines, and sewer work, with rules applied across the state. DGR Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and inspections in Fort Smith as part of our service.

    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer.
