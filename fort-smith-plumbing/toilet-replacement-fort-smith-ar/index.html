    Standard Toilet Replacement in Fort Smith

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. That is when we swap them. The old unit comes off, the flange gets scraped clean and checked for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Fort Smith

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Fort Smith, AR

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. You get repair and replacement prices next to each other. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Fort Smith, AR

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. Rough-in and bowl shape decide what fits, and we check both before you buy.

    What a Standard Swap Includes in Fort Smith

    The swap itself runs about two hours: water off, tank drained, old toilet out, flange scraped and inspected, new toilet set on a fresh seal, and a braided stainless supply line connected. Then we level it. The base gets caulked, with a small gap left at the back so any future leak shows up instead of hiding under the floor.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Fort Smith

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. We set it level with shims if the floor is out, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    ADA and Commercial Toilet Replacement in Fort Smith, AR

    Public restrooms need bowls rated for heavy use, and many need to meet ADA height and clearance rules when a fixture is replaced. Clearances get checked before we order anything. Adding a sensor flush valve during the swap takes little extra time, and they cut down on unflushed bowls.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Fort Smith, AR

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. We can usually replace it the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.
