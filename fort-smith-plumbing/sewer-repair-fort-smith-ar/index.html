    Sewer Pipe Relining in Fort Smith, AR

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Fort Smith.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Fort Smith

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's far more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Fort Smith dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with persistent drain issues.
