    Drop-In Sink Installation in Fort Smith, AR

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Fort Smith, AR

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Moving the drain adds the most. We price each piece on the quote.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Fort Smith, AR

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. We read the plan review first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Fort Smith

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Fort Smith, AR

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Fort Smith, AR

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. We check the spec sheet first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Fort Smith

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. Silicone holds in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries a full bowl. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Fort Smith
