    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Fort Smith

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace failed parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are correct.

    Pilot Light and Ignition Repair in Fort Smith, AR

    For gas-fired tankless water heaters, a malfunctioning ignition system can completely stop hot water production. Our team in Fort Smith, AR diagnoses issues with igniters, flame sensors and gas valves. We confirm proper gas pressure and ventilation are maintained for safe and efficient operation.     Condensing Tankless Repair in Fort Smith, AR
