    Hydro Jetting in Fort Smith

    Hydro jetting sends water at up to 4,000 psi through a specialized nozzle that scours the full inside wall of the pipe. Unlike a cable, which punches a hole through the clog, jetting removes grease, scale and fine roots completely. It is the go-to method for restaurant drains and lines that clog again within months. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough for jetting. Most residential jetting jobs take about two to three hours.

    Sewer Cleaning in Fort Smith, AR

If your home does not have an accessible cleanout, we can pull a toilet or install a new one. Main line clearing typically takes one to two hours.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Services in Fort Smith

    The cost of drain services in Fort Smith, AR can vary a great deal, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $5,000 for a major sewer line replacement. Factors like the type of blockage, its location, and the method required (for example, snaking versus hydro jetting) all influence the final price. We provide clear, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Most single fixture drain cleanings take under an hour. Main line cleaning and hydro jetting typically take one to three hours.

    Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the interior surfaces of your pipes, thoroughly removing grease, scale, and even tree roots. It is safe for most modern plumbing systems. Our technicians will perform a camera inspection first to ensure your pipes are structurally sound enough to withstand the process.

    Avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and be mindful of what gets flushed down toilets. Regular preventative maintenance, like an annual professional drain cleaning, can also be highly effective.

    Routine drain cleaning and jetting do not require permits. Sewer line repair or replacement typically does, and we handle those permits.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains, foul odors coming from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and multiple fixtures draining slowly. If you notice any of these, it's smart to call a professional before the problem escalates to a full blockage or a sewage backup.

    Hair, grease, soap scum and hard water scale cause most fixture clogs. Main line clogs are often caused by wipes, tree roots or a sag in the pipe.

    While readily available, we usually advise against using chemical drain cleaners. They can be corrosive, potentially damaging your pipes, especially older ones, and are often ineffective against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease. They also pose a risk to your health and the environment. Professional methods are safer for your pipes and more effective.
