    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in Fort Smith

    Pricing depends on the job. A leaky faucet or running toilet usually runs $150 to $350, a water heater installation is often $1,200 to $3,500, and slab leak repairs or full repipes can reach several thousand dollars. You get an exact quote after we diagnose.

    Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. A softener swaps those minerals out through ion exchange, and the difference shows up as scale in water heaters, white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and soap that will not lather well. Soft water is easier on fixtures.

    A plumber is licensed for water supply, drainage, water heating and gas work in a home, including repairs, installs and inspections. A drain cleaning company usually just clears clogs. DGR Plumbing does both, so if the clog came from a sagging line or a bad fitting, the same visit can fix the cause.

    Emergencies go to the top of the board. Our goal is a licensed plumber at your door within a few hours, and for a burst pipe or sewage backup we will walk you through closing the main shutoff on the phone so the damage stops while we drive. Keep towels ready.

    Soft water and normal pressure prevent a lot of what we see. Keep grease out of the drains and test pressure once a year, since anything over 80 psi wears out supply hoses and fixtures faster than people expect. An annual check finds small issues early.

    Every one to two years works for most homes in Fort Smith, AR. The inspection usually runs about an hour, and we check pressure, look for slow leaks at shutoffs and supply lines, test the water heater relief valve and look for corrosion on exposed pipe. Problems found early stay cheap.

    Gurgling is the early one. Slow drains, sewer smells and water backing up in a shower are the others, and when more than one fixture acts up at the same time, the clog is usually in the main line rather than a single trap. Main line clogs rarely clear on their own.

    Most repairs take one to three hours. A leaky pipe or clogged drain is often done in under two, while water heater installs take most of a day and full repipes can stretch over several days depending on the size of the house and access. We give you a timeline up front.
