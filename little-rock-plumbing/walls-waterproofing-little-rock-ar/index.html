    Wall Waterproofing Work We Do in Little Rock, AR

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Little Rock

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Rocky, shallow soil in hilly parts of Arkansas makes trenching harder and can raise the cost on sewer and water line replacements. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Little Rock, AR

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the level and wait 24 hours. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a two-piece clamping drain set on the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Little Rock, AR

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago. Newer homes across Arkansas mostly use PEX and PVC, which resist corrosion but still benefit from proper support and freeze protection. Fieldstone and old brick foundations need breathable products. Sealing soft old brick with a rigid coating traps water inside it and makes the face spall off over a few seasons, so we use lime-compatible or vapor-open coatings and pair them with a new interior drain. Old mortar joints get repointed first.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Little Rock, AR

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. Your quote lists each option with its price.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Little Rock, AR

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. Without one, the discharge water runs back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed far enough out that it drains away from the house.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Little Rock

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Little Rock

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Little Rock, AR

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. You get the options in writing, side by side.
