    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Little Rock

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Little Rock, AR

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. The test procedure is the same as for natural gas.

    CSST Repair in Little Rock

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. Bonding gets checked on every system. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Little Rock, AR

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Little Rock

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. Usually under an hour per appliance. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Little Rock

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Gas Leak Detection in Little Rock

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source, then confirm with soap solution at fittings, valves and unions. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.
