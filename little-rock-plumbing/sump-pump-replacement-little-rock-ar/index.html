    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Little Rock

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. Every pump we install gets a new check valve. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Pedestal Pump Service in Little Rock, AR

    Louder, yes, but a pedestal motor sits high and dry, so in a narrow pit it often outlasts a submersible by several years. Narrow pits favor pedestals. We replace worn units and set the float rod stops for a proper cycle. If your pit is wide enough, a submersible may serve you better.

    Backup Pump Options in Little Rock, AR

    River flooding affects low-lying parts of Arkansas, and floodwater can contaminate wells and swamp septic fields. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. The backup kicks on if the primary dies or the power goes out. Water-powered backups use city water pressure and need no battery, but they only make sense with strong municipal pressure and an approved backflow preventer on the line. They will not work on a well.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Little Rock

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Little Rock, AR

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want.     Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test.
