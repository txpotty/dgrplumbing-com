    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Little Rock

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Little Rock

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Little Rock, AR

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one.
