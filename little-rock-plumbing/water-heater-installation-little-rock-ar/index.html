    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Little Rock

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. Sizing is based on the first-hour rating on the yellow label, which tells you how much hot water the tank can deliver during a busy hour. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Little Rock, AR

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Each has its own install manual, and following it to the letter is what keeps the tank warranty valid if something fails in year six. We know those manuals well. We help you compare models on warranty length and recovery rate.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Little Rock, AR

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. When the water heats and expands with nowhere to go, pressure climbs and stresses the relief valve, the toilet fill valves and every supply line in the house. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Little Rock

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Little Rock

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. We install commercial tanks over 100,000 BTU and racked tankless systems, with the venting and gas supply those loads require. Downtime costs you money. Night installs are available.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Little Rock, AR

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. Gas tankless installs often run higher because of the bigger gas line and new venting, and attic jobs, relocations or code upgrades add labor on top of that. Your quote lists permits. We compare operating costs so you can choose with real numbers.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Little Rock, AR

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Little Rock, AR

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. A drain pan goes under closet installs. Quiet and simple.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Little Rock

    A tank heater usually lasts 8 to 12 years with maintenance. Tankless units often go 15 to 20 years because they do not sit full of hot water and their parts can be replaced.

    For two to four people, a 40 or 50 gallon tank usually does the job. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.
