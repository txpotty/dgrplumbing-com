    What We Install in Little Rock, AR

    Water Softener Installation in Little Rock, AR

    Homeowners near Little Rock on well water benefit from periodic testing and the right filter to protect pipes and appliances. Scale shows up first as white crust on faucet aerators and a rumbling water heater. We test hardness in grains per gallon at the kitchen tap, then size a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner to your household use. The bypass valve is not optional. It lets you shut the unit off for service without losing water to the house.

    Sump Pump Installation in Little Rock, AR

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Where the water exits matters most. The discharge gets carried well away from the foundation so the same water is not pumped twice.

    Water Heater Installation in Little Rock

    Tank or tankless, gas or electric, the first question is how many people shower in the morning. We size the heater to that peak demand and your fuel source, then check the venting and gas supply before quoting. Gas heaters need the closest look. Bad flue pitch or a spilling draft hood can send carbon monoxide into the living space. We haul the old tank away the same day.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Little Rock, AR

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. CSST must be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Little Rock, AR

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. Brands we trust here are Liberty and Zoeller.

    Gas Line Installation in Little Rock, AR

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. From there we size the pipe, choose between black iron and CSST, and dig the trench for any buried section. Every joint gets a soap test. After that, the line sits on a pressure gauge until the inspector signs off.

    Gas Line Installation for New Appliances in Little Rock, AR

    Water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces and pool heaters all pull from the same gas service. We add up the BTU demand of everything on the line, measure the run, and size each branch so the furthest appliance still gets full pressure. Every new line is pressure tested. Bigger loads sometimes need a meter upgrade, and we set that up with the gas utility.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Little Rock, AR

    A closed water system has nowhere for heated water to expand, so pressure climbs every single time the heater cycles. The expansion tank takes up that swing so the relief valve stops weeping. A PRV usually means you need one. For basement baths and guest suites below the sewer, we also install ejector systems with a sealed basin, check valve and vent.
