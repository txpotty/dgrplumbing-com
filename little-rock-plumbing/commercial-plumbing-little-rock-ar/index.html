    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Little Rock

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Rocky, shallow soil in hilly parts of Arkansas makes trenching harder and can add time on sewer and water line replacements. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. You get a price on each option.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Little Rock

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Little Rock, AR

Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.
