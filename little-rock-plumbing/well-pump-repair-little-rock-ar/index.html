    Control Box and Surge Repair in Little Rock, AR

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Severe storms and heavy spring rain are common in Arkansas, and saturated ground can overwhelm sump pumps and flood sewer lines. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Common problems are loss of prime, a leaking shaft seal and a worn impeller.
