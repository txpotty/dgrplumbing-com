    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Little Rock

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot get into. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Little Rock, AR

    While the upfront cost of trenchless sewer repair in Little Rock, AR can sometimes be comparable to or slightly higher than traditional methods, the overall savings are often substantial. This is because trenchless repairs eliminate the need for extensive excavation, backfilling and landscape restoration. Homeowners save money on repairing driveways, patios and gardens, making it a more sensible choice in the long run for many.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Little Rock, AR

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a quick and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Arkansas homeowners.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Little Rock

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Little Rock, AR

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Little Rock

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Little Rock that require full replacement rather than just relining.
