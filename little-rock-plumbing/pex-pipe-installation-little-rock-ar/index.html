    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Little Rock

    Radiant floor loops are PEX too, but a different kind you drink from. Heating loops need oxygen-barrier PEX, which has a coating that keeps oxygen from migrating through the wall and rusting the boiler, the pump and any iron in the system. Loops have length limits.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Little Rock

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and the honest answer depends on the job. PEX-A makes tighter bends, can be heat-fixed if it kinks, and its fittings flow better, while PEX-B is cheaper and its crimps hold just as well when each one is gauged. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Little Rock

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. Since a crimp is only as good as the tool that made it, every ring gets a go/no-go gauge check, and any ring that fails gets cut off and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.

    Commercial PEX Pipe Installation in Little Rock

    Commercial PEX work in Little Rock runs on the business schedule, not ours.
