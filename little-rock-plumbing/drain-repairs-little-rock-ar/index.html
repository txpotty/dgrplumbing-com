    Bathroom Drain Repair in Little Rock

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Scale speeds it up. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, plus tub and shower drain leaks that stain the room underneath. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Little Rock, AR

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Little Rock

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Main Line Drain Repair in Little Rock

    A problem in the main line shows up at every drain in the house. Basement and first-floor drains go first. Roots, cracks from ground movement and collapsed sections are the common causes, and we confirm which one on camera, then repair it with a spot dig, a trenchless liner or a section replacement depending on what the footage shows. We show you before we quote.

    Storm Drain Repair in Little Rock, AR

    Yard and storm drains carry rain away from the foundation. A battery backup sump pump and a backwater valve are practical upgrades for homes that have taken on water before. Leaves and silt clog them. We clear the line with a jetter, camera it to look for crushed or separated sections, and repair broken pipe so the runoff reaches daylight instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. A clean outlet matters too.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Little Rock

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. The grease goes far down the pipe. We replace cracked tailpieces, fix slip nuts and correct trap height, then jet the branch line and check its fall when the buildup sits past the wall. Dishwasher and disposal connections get checked.

    Drain Vent Repair in Little Rock, AR

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. The drain cannot breathe. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, which is why we inspect the roof opening and the vent piping inside the walls. Clearing it is often quick.

Any access hole is kept small and neat.
