    What Our Little Rock, AR Sewer Line Techs Handle

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Little Rock, AR

    Jetting cleans the pipe wall. Cables only open a path. A jetter scours the full circumference of the pipe at pressures we set for the material, from PVC to clay.

    Large Commercial Laterals in Little Rock, AR

    Big commercial laterals carry more solids. Kitchens add grease. Our larger jetters handle big commercial pipe, focusing on interceptor outlets and long laterals, plus written reports for maintenance logs.

    Jetting Heavy Buildup in Little Rock

    Some lines need more than a cable. That is jetting work. Water at up to 4,000 PSI scours grease, scale, mineral deposits and roots off the pipe wall, cleaning the full diameter instead of punching a hole through the blockage.

    Tree Root Removal from Sewer Lines in Little Rock

    Roots enter through small cracks and loose joints. Expansive clay soils across much of Arkansas swell and shrink with rainfall, which can stress foundations and crack buried sewer lines. They grow fast inside. We cut roots out with a saw head, then flush the line with a jetter to clear what the saw leaves behind, and we explain options to keep them out.

    Commercial Sewer Line Cleaning in Little Rock, AR

    Commercial laterals see heavy use. Kitchens add grease every shift. Our larger cables and jetters handle commercial lines, and we work around your hours so customers are not affected.

    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Little Rock, AR

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Digging costs far more. If the pipe is structurally sound, regular cleaning is often the better value, but if the camera shows a collapse or a long belly, cleaning only buys time, and you see both prices.

    Sewer Line Cleaning for Rentals and HOAs in Little Rock

    Rentals see more clogs from wipes and grease. Turnover adds to it. We offer scheduled cleanings for landlords and property managers, and for HOAs we clean shared lines and document each building so the budget can be planned, coordinating access with tenants.
