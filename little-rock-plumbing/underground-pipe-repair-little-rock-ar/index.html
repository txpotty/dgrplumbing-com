    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Little Rock

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Little Rock

    A single repair on a buried water line typically costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. Full main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on how deep the pipe is.

    Trenchless pipe repair offers several key advantages, including minimal disruption to your yard, landscaping and concrete, which can save on restoration costs. It's usually faster than traditional excavation and less invasive, preserving the look and value of your property. This method is also durable, frequently extending the lifespan of your pipes.

    Many water utilities offer a one-time leak adjustment if you repair the leak promptly and provide proof. We give you an invoice and repair documentation to support your request.

    Look for unusually wet spots in your yard, unexplained increases in your water bill, foul odors, decreased water pressure or gurgling sounds from drains. These can all indicate a hidden leak or blockage in your underground plumbing system.

    For underground repairs, we usually use durable materials like PVC, HDPE for trenchless methods or occasionally copper for certain water lines. These materials resist corrosion, root intrusion and ground shifts, providing a long-lasting solution that meets Arkansas plumbing standards.

    Whether an underground pipe leak is covered by insurance depends on your homeowner's policy and the cause of the damage. Typically, sudden and accidental damage may be covered, but issues from normal wear and tear or neglect are usually not. It's best to review your policy or contact your insurance provider directly for clarification.

    DGR Plumbing is equipped to repair a wide range of underground pipe materials, including PVC, ABS, cast iron, copper, PEX and even older materials like clay or polybutylene. Our technicians are trained on the specific repair techniques for each material.

    In most cities, the homeowner owns and maintains the water line from the meter to the house. The utility is usually responsible for the meter and the line on the street side. We can help confirm which side the leak is on.
