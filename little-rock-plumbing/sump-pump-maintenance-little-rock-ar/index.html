    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Little Rock

    A standard sump pump inspection and cleaning typically runs $125 to $250. Replacing a float switch or check valve usually adds $75 to $250 depending on parts.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This shows the importance of maintaining backup systems.

    Most quality pumps last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit dry for long periods can seize sooner, which is why testing matters during dry months.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more worthwhile. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.

    While you can perform basic checks like making sure the pump is plugged in and the discharge pipe isn't blocked, professional maintenance goes much deeper. Our technicians can spot subtle electrical issues, float switch malfunctions or impeller damage that a DIY check might miss. We recommend professional service for thoroughness and safety.

    Most local codes do not allow sump discharge into the sanitary sewer. We route discharge to the yard, a dry well or another approved location.

    If your basement is finished or storms often knock out your power, a backup is a smart choice. We install and maintain battery and water-powered backup systems.

    Watch out for odd noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's clearly time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.
