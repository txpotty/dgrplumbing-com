    Sump Pump Maintenance in Little Rock, AR: What We Check

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Little Rock, AR

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Elevator Pit and Garage Pumps in Little Rock

    Elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms all collect water that has nowhere to go by gravity, so a pump has to lift it out. In elevator pits, the pump usually needs an oil-sensing control so it will not discharge hydraulic fluid. We test duplex systems and high-water alarms on a set schedule. Logs go to the property manager.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Little Rock, AR

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. We test both pumps, the alternator and the high-water alarm. Property managers get a written log of every reading we take, which helps at insurance renewal and during code inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Home Sump Pump Service in Little Rock

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. Our plumber lifts the pump out, cleans the screen and empties the sediment from the bottom. After that, the pump gets a real water test. Rocky, shallow soil in hilly parts of Arkansas makes trenching harder and can add time on sewer and water line replacements.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Little Rock, AR

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Crawlspaces and deep window wells often have small pumps that clear runoff after a storm. We clean their screens, test the floats and follow the discharge until it reaches daylight or an approved drain, because a line that dead-ends under the house just moves the water around. Hard freezes hit Arkansas most winters, especially in the northern and higher areas, and exposed pipes can split during a sharp cold snap.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Little Rock, AR

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. Those units need more frequent checks than a house pump. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Little Rock

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. With the pit clean, the float swings without catching. The pump runs cooler too.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Little Rock

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.
