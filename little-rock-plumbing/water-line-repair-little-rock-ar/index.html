    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Little Rock

    Slab leaks, where water lines running beneath your home's concrete foundation leak, are a especially challenging issue. Older homes in Arkansas often still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, both of which corrode from the inside with age. These leaks can undermine your foundation, leading to major structural damage. We use specialized listening devices and camera inspections to pinpoint slab leaks precisely and offer repair options including direct slab penetration, re-routing, or pipe lining. Addressing a slab leak promptly in Little Rock is crucial to prevent extensive, costly foundation damage.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Little Rock, AR

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water. Our emergency crews shut off the supply, find the break and set up a temporary bypass when the repair will take more than a few hours. Most emergency breaks are repaired the same day.     Water Line Repair at the Meter in Little Rock, AR

    Leaks often start at the compression fittings and couplings right next to the meter box. Shifting soil and heavy traffic over the box can work loose those joints over time. We determine whether the leak is on the utility side or the customer side, since the water provider is in charge of the meter and the pipe before it. When the leak is yours, we replace the fittings and the customer shutoff valve in the same visit.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Little Rock, AR

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Little Rock, AR. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make solid, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform well.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Little Rock

    If your service line runs under a driveway, pool deck or mature landscaping, trenchless replacement can save a lot of restoration cost. We dig small pits at each end and pull new HDPE or PEX pipe through using a pneumatic boring tool or pipe pulling equipment. The process typically takes one day. You keep your pavers and trees, and the new line carries a lengthy manufacturer warranty.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Little Rock

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred. Common signs include sudden spikes in your water bill, damp spots in your yard, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. Our team uses acoustic leak detection and ground-penetrating radar to accurately pinpoint these hidden leaks in Little Rock, reducing the need for extensive excavation. We repair breaks caused by shifting soil, tree root intrusion, or aging pipes, restoring your water service efficiently.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Little Rock

    Copper pipes are known for their durability and long life, but they can still develop leaks due to corrosion, excessive water pressure, or physical damage. In Little Rock, we often see pinhole leaks in older copper lines. Our skilled plumbers are adept at repairing copper water lines, whether it involves soldering a small patch or replacing a damaged section. We make sure all repairs meet current plumbing standards for a reliable, leak-free connection.
