    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Little Rock

    Pedestal sump pumps are an good choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can often offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. DGR Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Little Rock home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Little Rock

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels usually need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm alerts building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a clear maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.

    New Sump Pit Excavation in Little Rock

    If your basement has never had a pump, we start by finding the low point where water collects. We saw cut the slab, excavate a pit roughly 18 to 24 inches wide and 24 to 30 inches deep, and set a perforated or solid liner depending on whether we are collecting groundwater or surface seepage. Gravel goes around the liner to help water flow in while keeping fine soil out. We then patch the slab flush with new concrete so the floor remains clean and level.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Little Rock, AR

    Power outages are common, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that automatically activate if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Little Rock, AR. Severe storms and heavy spring rain are common in Arkansas, and saturated ground can overwhelm sump pumps and back up sewer lines. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Little Rock, AR

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, which makes them quieter and less intrusive. They are powerful and effective for basements that take on a lot of water. Our team in Little Rock, AR will install your submersible pump with precision, ensuring proper sealing, discharge piping and electrical connections for quiet, reliable operation, which matters most in finished basements.
