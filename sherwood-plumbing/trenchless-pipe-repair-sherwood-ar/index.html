    Perma-Liner and Picote Solutions in Sherwood

    Perma-Liner supplies the lining systems and Picote makes the cutters. Both matter. Picote milling heads clear roots, scale and old fittings out of the pipe before lining, and after the liner cures, they cut open the lateral connections so every drain in your home flows again. A clean pipe holds a liner best.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Sherwood

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Sherwood yard.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Sherwood

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. Its liners cure into a structural pipe that holds even if the old host pipe keeps falling apart around it. They handle harsh soil and flow.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in Sherwood

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. Then the liner goes in. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one. Lining works best for cracks, root intrusion and separated joints.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Sherwood, AR

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. Bursting or boring works. Our crew pulls new HDPE or PEX through the old path with a bursting head, or drills a fresh path with a directional boring tool, so lawns, driveways and walkways stay untouched. The line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Pipe Bursting in Sherwood, AR

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. The head fractures the old pipe as it travels, with new HDPE trailing behind, fused into one length with no joints.

    Sectional Point Repair in Sherwood

    Sometimes only one joint or a few feet of pipe is bad. A spot liner fixes that. Guided by camera, a short liner is placed over the bad section and cured, so you pay for a few feet instead of the full line. It suits isolated damage in Sherwood laterals.

    Sewer Lateral Lining in Sherwood

Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe crack, sag or fill with roots over time, so we jet the line clean, inspect it by camera and choose lining or bursting based on what we see. The new line restores full flow and stands up to roots.
