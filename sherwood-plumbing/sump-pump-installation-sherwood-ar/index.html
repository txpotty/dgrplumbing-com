    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Sherwood, AR

    A pump replacement in an existing pit typically costs much less than a new pit installation. New pits require slab cutting, excavation and concrete patching, which adds labor.     Primary Sump Pump Installation in Sherwood

    A primary sump pump is your first defense against basement flooding. We install sturdy, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit. Our plumbers will assess your basement's water intrusion risks, considering local water tables and soil types in Sherwood, to recommend the right pump size and horsepower for effective water removal.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Sherwood

    A high water alarm alerts you when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails.     New Sump Pit Installation in Sherwood

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Sherwood, AR

    For complete peace of mind, consider a combination sump pump system. This setup includes a primary electric sump pump and an integrated battery backup pump in a single unit, providing full protection against both power failures and primary pump malfunctions. DGR Plumbing can install these dual-pump systems in your Sherwood, AR home, offering superior flood prevention. This solution is a good fit for homes in flood-prone areas or those with expensive basement finishes.
