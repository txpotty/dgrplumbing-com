    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Sherwood

    Regular inspections are the cornerstone of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This proactive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Sherwood

    The control valve is the brain of your water softener, dictating regeneration cycles and water flow. Our maintenance service includes complete diagnostics of the control valve's programming and mechanical components. We check for proper timing, motor function and potential blockages. If issues are found, we can often repair or replace worn parts to restore full function, avoiding the need for a costly full system replacement in Sherwood.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Sherwood, AR

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Annual Maintenance Plans in Sherwood, AR

    For lasting peace of mind and long-term savings, consider our annual water softener maintenance plans. These plans ensure your system receives regular, scheduled check-ups, often including priority scheduling and discounts on repairs. It's the best way to protect your investment and keep soft water flowing in your Sherwood, AR home or business.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Sherwood, AR

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Sherwood

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we analyze the condition of the resin beads and confirm the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to consistent soft water throughout your home in Sherwood.

    Water Softener Brand Servicing in Sherwood

    DGR Plumbing has broad experience maintaining all major water softener brands, including Culligan, Kenmore, Whirlpool, Fleck, Kinetico and more. Our technicians are familiar with the specific components and maintenance requirements of each manufacturer. Whether you have an older model or a brand-new high-efficiency unit, you can trust us to provide careful maintenance that keeps your system operating smoothly in Sherwood.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Sherwood

    Softener resin can foul with iron, manganese or sediment and lose capacity over time. Chlorine and chloramine in city water also slowly break resin down. We run a resin cleaner through the system and test performance afterward. If the resin is exhausted, we rebed the tank with fresh high-capacity resin, which costs far less than a new system.
