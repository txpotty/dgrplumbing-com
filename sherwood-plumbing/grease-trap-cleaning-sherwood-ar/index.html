    Grease Trap Pumping and Hauling in Sherwood

    Trap waste is regulated as a liquid waste, so in most places it has to be hauled by a licensed hauler to an approved receiving facility, not dumped down a manhole or into a dumpster.     Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in Sherwood

    Most pretreatment programs use the 25 percent rule: pump before floating grease and settled solids together take up a quarter of the trap's liquid depth. Some cities set a fixed interval instead, often 90 days for interceptors. Whichever rule your program uses, we schedule to it. Changes are easy.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Sherwood, AR

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. We open every compartment and pump each one to the floor. While the tank is empty, we look at the inlet and outlet tees, the baffle wall, the risers and the lid seals, because a cracked riser lets groundwater and dirt in. Lids go back on tight.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Sherwood

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. A hard grease cap gets broken up with a pressure wand before pumping, otherwise it plugs the suction hose and half of it stays in the tank. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Commercial Grease Trap Cleaning in Sherwood

    Restaurants and hospital kitchens in Sherwood run the same basic setup, a trap or interceptor sitting between the sinks and the sewer, sized by flow rate or by gallons. We pump it empty and scrape it. Before the lid goes back on, we look over both tees and the baffle wall for cracks or missing pieces. Intervals run anywhere from two weeks to three months.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Sherwood, AR

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. We run a jetter at roughly 3,000 to 4,000 psi with a rotating nozzle that cuts the grease off the full circumference of the pipe and flushes it downstream. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.

Gasket and flow control get checked too.
