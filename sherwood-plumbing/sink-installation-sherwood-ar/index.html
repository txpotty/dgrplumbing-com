    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Sherwood

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. Getting an undermount centered under a polished cutout so the reveal is even on all sides takes a helper, a few shims and patience before the clips get tightened. Uneven reveals look bad forever. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Sherwood, AR

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. A towel shows a weep fast. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Sherwood

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. Each bowl drains through its own trap or through a continuous waste to a shared trap, and the second faucet gets new stops. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Sherwood, AR

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Sherwood, AR
