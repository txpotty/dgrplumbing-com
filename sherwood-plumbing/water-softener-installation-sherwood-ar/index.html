    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Sherwood

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture.     Residential Water Softener Installation in Sherwood

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning.     High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Sherwood

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Sherwood, AR

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. Their parts are easy to find and the valves can be rebuilt instead of replaced, so the system lasts. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Sherwood

    Testing is what makes the settings right. Our crew checks hardness, iron and chlorine, then sets the regeneration and salt dosage to match. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Sherwood

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. Regeneration time is usually set for 2 a.m.
