    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Sherwood

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Sherwood, AR

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Sherwood, AR

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Tub Faucet Style Upgrades in Sherwood

    Wall-mount tub fillers are a popular upgrade. A long-reach filler or a slide-bar handheld changes the tub, and both usually need a new valve or diverter. Rough-in gets planned before trim. The trim goes on last.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Sherwood

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Sherwood, AR

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. Unlike a pressure-balance valve, which only reacts to pressure changes, a thermostatic valve senses the actual water temperature and adjusts the mix, which matters for kids and older adults who cannot react fast to a hot spike. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Sherwood, AR

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Sherwood, AR

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.
