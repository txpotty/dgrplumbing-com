    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Sherwood

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Sherwood

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Fused HDPE has no joints at all, so roots have nowhere to start.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Sherwood

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Sherwood

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. We say plainly whether your line qualifies.
