    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Sherwood, AR

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Before a deep freeze, disconnect hoses, drip a faucet on outside walls, and know where your main shutoff is located. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Sherwood, AR

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Sherwood, AR, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.
