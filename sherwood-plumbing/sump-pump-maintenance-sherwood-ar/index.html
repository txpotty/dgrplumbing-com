    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Maintenance in Sherwood

    The cost for sump pump maintenance in Sherwood, AR typically ranges from $100 to $250, depending on the pump type and its condition. This price generally includes a thorough inspection, cleaning and operational testing. Any necessary repairs or parts replacement would be an additional cost, which we would quote upfront.

    Most visits take 45 minutes to an hour. Replacing a check valve or float on the same visit adds a little time.

    Homeowners near Sherwood on well water benefit from periodic testing and the right filter to protect pipes and appliances. Where groundwater is hard, mineral deposits can build up on the pump's impeller and in the pit, reducing efficiency and potentially causing clogs or damage over time. Regular maintenance helps reduce these effects.

    We service Zoeller, Liberty, Wayne, Little Giant, Superior and most other residential and commercial pumps. We can also recommend upgrades for pits that need more pumping power.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, twice-yearly maintenance might be more beneficial. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.

    The check valve is essential because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short cycle, leading to premature wear and increased energy use. Our maintenance includes checking its function.

    Watch out for odd noises, such as grinding or rattling, that point to debris or a failing motor. If the pump runs constantly, fails to turn on or gives off a foul odor from the pit, it's definitely time for a professional check. Visible rust or corrosion are also red flags.

    If your primary sump pump fails during a power outage and you don't have a working battery backup or water-powered backup system, your basement is at risk of flooding. This shows the importance of maintaining backup systems.
