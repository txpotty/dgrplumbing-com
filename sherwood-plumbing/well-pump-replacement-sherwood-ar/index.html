    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Sherwood

    Well pump replacement usually costs $1,800 to $4,500 depending on well depth and pump size. Deep wells or constant pressure systems can cost $4,000 to $8,000.

    Most well pump replacements are completed in one day. Deep wells or complex systems may take longer.

    In many areas, permits may be required for major plumbing work like well pump replacement, especially if it involves changing the well system's electrical or structural components. DGR Plumbing will help you work through any permitting in Sherwood, AR so your installation is fully compliant with local regulations.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or surprisingly high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.

    New well pumps usually come with a manufacturer's warranty, usually ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. DGR Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty. We will discuss warranty details for your chosen pump during the quoting process.

    If your pressure tank is old or waterlogged, replacing it at the same time can protect the new pump. A failed tank causes rapid short cycling, which is one of the most common reasons submersible motors burn out early.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is highly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, avoiding costly mistakes.
