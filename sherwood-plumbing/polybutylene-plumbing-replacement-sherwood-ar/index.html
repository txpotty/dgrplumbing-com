    Why Polybutylene Fails in Sherwood

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see more mineral-heavy water from groundwater sources. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Sherwood

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. It also tolerates freezing better. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.
