    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Sherwood, AR

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a detailed estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Sherwood

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Emergency Plumbing Readiness in Sherwood, AR

    While maintenance aims to prevent emergencies, being prepared is still wise. Our services include checking for common causes of urgent issues, like exposed pipes that can freeze or early signs of slab leaks. Hard freezes hit Arkansas most winters, especially in the northern and higher areas, and exposed pipes can split during a sudden cold snap. Finding and fixing these weak spots during routine maintenance greatly reduces the chance of needing an emergency plumber or costly water damage restoration down the line.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Sherwood

    Older homes in Arkansas often still have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, both of which corrode from the inside with age. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Sherwood

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Sherwood

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.
