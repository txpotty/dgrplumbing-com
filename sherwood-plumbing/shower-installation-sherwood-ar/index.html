    Steam Shower Plumbing in Sherwood

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Digital Shower Systems in Sherwood, AR

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. The outlet is an electrician's job. Our part is the plumbing.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Sherwood

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Pressure varies by house. We test static pressure before designing the layout.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Sherwood, AR

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Sherwood

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Gym and Locker Room Showers in Sherwood

    Gym showers run back to back from open to close. Commercial valves with metered or push-button controls keep run times in check, and the drains are sized for heavy use. Accessible stalls get handhelds on slide bars. Work is phased so part of the room stays open.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Sherwood

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Sherwood

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Steel and acrylic tubs need a mortar bed or foam under them, and cast iron needs a level ledger, so the tub sits flat and the caulk joint at the wall does not crack. Then the valve. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Most of what we install is Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard or Sterling. We can also install a brand you have already picked out, as long as we can get the rough-in valve and the specs before rough-in day. Specs matter for depth.

    Both stop scalding, but they work differently. A pressure-balancing valve adjusts the hot and cold ratio when pressure changes elsewhere in the house, while a thermostatic valve holds an exact temperature no matter what the pressure does. Thermostatic costs more.

    A pressure-balancing valve watches pressure. If cold pressure drops from a flush, the valve reduces hot to keep the mix steady. Thermostatic valves sense the actual temperature. That makes it better for multi-head showers.
