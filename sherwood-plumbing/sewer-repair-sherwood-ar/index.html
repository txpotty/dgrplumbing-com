    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Sherwood, AR

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall.     Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Sherwood, AR

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and we locate the break and its depth before we start digging. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Sherwood, AR

    Roots enter through hairline cracks and loose joints, then grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. They come back fast.     Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Sherwood, AR

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Then the sewer smell shows up.     Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Sherwood, AR

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Expansive clay soils across much of Arkansas swell and shrink with rainfall, which can shift foundations and crack buried sewer lines. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.
