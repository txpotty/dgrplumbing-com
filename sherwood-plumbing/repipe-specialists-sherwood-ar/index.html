    Efficient PEX Repiping in Sherwood, AR

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Sherwood, AR. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also very resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.
