    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a popular choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing.     PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Bentonville, AR

    PEX and HDPE are flexible, which means fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement. HDPE is especially durable for long runs and resists cracking in expansive clay. PEX must stay out of sunlight before burial, so we store and lay it with care on every job site.
