    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Bentonville, AR

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Bentonville, AR

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Bentonville

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is particularly important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and potential breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Bentonville

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups.
