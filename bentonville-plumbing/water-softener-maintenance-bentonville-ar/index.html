    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Bentonville

    Regular inspections are the cornerstone of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This preventive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repair in Bentonville, AR

    A leaking bypass valve can let hard water slip past the softener without you noticing. We test and rebuild bypass valves and replace cracked fittings. We also inspect the drain line for kinks and proper air gap and the brine overflow line for correct routing. These small fixes head off leaks and backflow problems.

    Preventative Maintenance for All Brands in Bentonville, AR

    Whether you have a Fleck, Culligan, Kenmore, GE or EcoPure system, our technicians are trained to service all major water softener brands. Our preventative maintenance protocols are adapted to the specific requirements of each manufacturer, so your unit receives the best possible care, regardless of its make or model in Bentonville, AR.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Bentonville, AR

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a complete regeneration every time.

    Commercial Softener Maintenance in Bentonville

    Restaurants, laundromats, salons and car washes rely on soft water to protect equipment like dishwashers, ice machines and boilers. Commercial twin-tank systems regenerate often and need closer attention. We set up scheduled visits to test hardness, clean brine tanks and service valves. Consistent maintenance keeps equipment warranties valid and reduces repair costs.

    Regeneration Settings and Hardness Testing in Bentonville, AR

    Many softeners are set incorrectly from day one or never updated when household size changes. We test your raw water hardness and program the valve to match your actual use. Metered systems regenerate based on gallons used, which saves salt compared to time clock models. Correct settings keep water soft without wasting salt or water.

    Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Bentonville, AR

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is essential for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Bentonville, AR.
