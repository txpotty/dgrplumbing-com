    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Bentonville, AR

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off.     Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Bentonville

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to early wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Bentonville residence.
