    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Bentonville

    The cost for urinal installation in Bentonville, AR can range from $400 to $1,200, depending on the type of urinal, complexity of plumbing modifications, and whether it's a new installation or a replacement.     A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more strongly down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    A replacement takes two to four hours. A new install with rough-in and carrier typically takes one to two days, plus inspection time.

    Switching from a 1 gpf urinal to a 0.125 gpf model saves most of the water per flush. In a busy restroom that adds up to many thousands of gallons a year.

    While it might seem like a way to save money, urinal installation involves precise plumbing connections, proper venting, and secure mounting that are best handled by a professional.
