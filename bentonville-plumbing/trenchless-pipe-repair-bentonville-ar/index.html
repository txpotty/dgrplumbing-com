    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Bentonville

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. The resin cures in place. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Bentonville yard.

    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Bentonville

    Older Bentonville homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. They wear out. Clay joints let roots in and cast iron scales and rusts through, and a liner seals both problems with a new jointless pipe that often outlasts the original material by decades. Only one or two access points are needed.
