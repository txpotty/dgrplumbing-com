    The gas control valve controls temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something goes wrong.     Venting and Draft Repair in Pine Bluff, AR

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home.     Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Pine Bluff

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.
