    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Pine Bluff, AR

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Pine Bluff, AR

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water.
