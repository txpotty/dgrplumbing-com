    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Pine Bluff

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Pine Bluff

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage. If your main water line in Pine Bluff has multiple leaks, severe corrosion, or is nearing the end of its lifespan, replacement is often the more sensible long-term solution. Our technicians will perform a complete inspection, including pressure testing and possibly video inspection, to recommend the best course of action. We consider factors like the age of your home, the material of the existing pipe, and the nature of the damage before advising.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Pine Bluff, AR

    Beyond simple replacement, we also offer water line upgrades to increase water flow or ensure compliance with current plumbing codes.     Copper Service Line Installation in Pine Bluff, AR

    Type K copper is a durable option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is favored by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to cushion it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    Commercial properties often have larger service lines and backflow assemblies. We replace commercial water lines and coordinate with the utility when needed.
