    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Pine Bluff

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Even a thin crack will leak. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Wall-Hung Commercial Bowl Replacement in Pine Bluff, AR

    Wall-hung commercial bowls sit on steel carriers inside the wall, and when one cracks we also check that the carrier bolts and the outlet gasket are still sound. Loose carriers crack bowls. Flushometer valves get reconnected and tested before anyone uses the restroom again.
