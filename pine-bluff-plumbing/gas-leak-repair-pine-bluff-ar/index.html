    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Pine Bluff

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. Fence posts and sprinkler trenches hit those lines more often than corrosion does. Dead grass is a tell. The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Pine Bluff, AR

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. The fix is usually a new coated connector and a new ball valve, plus a drip leg if the appliance lacks one. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is relit.

    Emergency Gas Leak Services in Pine Bluff

    That sulfur smell is mercaptan, an odorant the supplier adds because natural gas and propane have almost no smell on their own. A strong smell means walk out and leave the door open.     Residential Gas Leak Detection in Pine Bluff

    A gas leak in a house rarely sits where the smell is strongest, because gas drifts along joists and collects in closets and basements. The first step is a pressure gauge on the piping, which tells us whether the system is actually losing gas. Then the sniffer comes out. A combustible gas detector follows the reading from fitting to fitting until it spikes. Bubble solution on the suspect joint gives the final answer.

    Gas Line Replacement and Upgrades in Pine Bluff, AR

    Adding a gas range or a tankless heater can overload pipe that was sized years ago for just a furnace and a water heater. Sizing starts with the total BTU load and the longest run from the meter. Undersized pipe starves burners. Where the old pipe is rusty or poorly threaded, we replace it with black iron or CSST, whichever reaches easier. A big tankless unit may also need a larger meter from the utility.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Pine Bluff

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. If your range or water heater still has one, it should come out. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.
