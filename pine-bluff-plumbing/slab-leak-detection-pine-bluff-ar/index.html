    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Pine Bluff, AR

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab.     Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Pine Bluff, AR

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair.     Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Pine Bluff, AR

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic.
