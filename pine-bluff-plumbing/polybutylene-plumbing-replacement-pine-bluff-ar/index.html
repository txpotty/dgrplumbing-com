    Why Polybutylene Fails in Pine Bluff

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see harder water from groundwater sources. Heat makes it worse. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

When the service line is already sound, we tie new PEX into it at the main with a listed transition fitting and skip the trenching. We check the service line first. Blue or black poly service pipe should come out as well.
