    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. Our crews follow them.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Pine Bluff

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
