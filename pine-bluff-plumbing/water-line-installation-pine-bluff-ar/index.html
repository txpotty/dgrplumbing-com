    Main Water Line Replacement in Pine Bluff

    Over time, main water lines can corrode, crack or leak because of shifting soil, tree root intrusion or age. DGR Plumbing specializes in replacing old, failing main water lines, often upgrading from galvanized steel or polybutylene to more durable PEX or copper.     PEX Water Line Installation in Pine Bluff

    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a common choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Big temperature swings between seasons expand and contract pipes and fittings, which can loosen older joints over time. DGR Plumbing professionally installs PEX water lines for both new construction and replacements in Pine Bluff. PEX offers excellent flow rates and is less prone to bursting than traditional rigid pipe, providing a long-lasting, cost-effective solution for your home's plumbing.

    Upsizing Water Lines for Better Pressure in Pine Bluff, AR

    Many homes built decades ago have a 3/4 inch line that cannot keep up with modern fixture loads, irrigation and pools. If your pressure drops when two showers run or the sprinklers are on, an undersized line may be the culprit. We test static and flowing pressure, then calculate whether a larger line will make a real difference. Upsizing is frequently paired with a new pressure reducing valve and main shutoff.

    New Construction Water Service Lines in Pine Bluff

    New homes, guest houses and additions need a correctly sized service line from the meter to the building. We review the plans, confirm meter size with your water provider and choose a line diameter that matches the fixture load. Most homes need a 3/4 inch or 1 inch line, while larger homes with pools and big irrigation systems often benefit from 1 1/4 inch. We coordinate the rough-in timing with your builder so the line is ready for inspection.
