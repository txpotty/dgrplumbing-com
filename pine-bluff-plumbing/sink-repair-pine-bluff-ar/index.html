    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Pine Bluff

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout.     Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Pine Bluff, AR

    Bathroom Sink Repair in Pine Bluff, AR

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Pine Bluff

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. We reset the bowl on new clips. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.
