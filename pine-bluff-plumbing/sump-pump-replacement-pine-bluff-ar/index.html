    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Pine Bluff, AR

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Pine Bluff, AR, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Pine Bluff, AR

    For added protection, many homeowners in Pine Bluff choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump malfunctions. DGR Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are properly matched for strong performance and longevity.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Pine Bluff

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Pine Bluff, AR

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. DGR Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Pine Bluff, AR so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is important for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.
