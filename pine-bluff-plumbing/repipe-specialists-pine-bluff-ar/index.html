    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Pine Bluff, AR

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Pine Bluff

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Pine Bluff

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Pine Bluff, AR

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Pine Bluff, AR

    Many municipal systems in Arkansas draw from lakes and reservoirs that supply fairly soft water, though some areas see harder water from groundwater sources. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Pine Bluff

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Pine Bluff, AR

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Pine Bluff, AR

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Pine Bluff, AR

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.
