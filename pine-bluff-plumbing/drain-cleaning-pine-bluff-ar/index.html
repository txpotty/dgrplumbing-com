    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    Hydro jetting is usually safe for most modern plumbing systems. However, it may not suit severely corroded or fragile pipes, which are sometimes found in very old Pine Bluff properties. We always perform a camera inspection first to assess pipe condition and use the safest method.

    Typically, you do not need a permit for routine drain cleaning or unclogging a drain. Permits are generally required for more extensive plumbing work, such as replacing a sewer line or making major changes to your plumbing system. We will advise you if any permit is needed for your situation.
